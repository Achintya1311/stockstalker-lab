## Where it works and fails

- **"Oversold" is not a bounce signal here.** Day bullish is negative in four of six years. The same reading
  appears in T12 (oversold up-cross, -4.53, `t12.md`). T04's RSI(2) bullish side is the exception on edge
  (+3.37), but its gross move is about zero (`t04.md`), so no oversold reading in the lab pays its cost.
- **"Overbought" fades mildly**, in the same direction as T06's upper-band fade (+3.35, `t06.md`) and T13's
  Williams %R fade (+2.89, `t13.md`), but at about a third of their size.
  Overlap of T03's day-bearish top five: 30% with
  T13's, 25% with T06's, 5% with E2A's.
- **Costs:** every side's directional move after costs is negative. The best, day bearish at -0.04%, has a
  gross move of about +0.21% against the 0.25% futures cost.
