## Where it works and fails

- **A two-year signal.** The bearish average comes from 2016 and 2020 (+5.91, +6.10); 2017-18, the years mid caps fell
  while a few large caps rose, are both about -3.4. The same 2017-18 weakness as H01's wrong-way bearish reading.
- **Independent but empty.** Bearish top-5 overlap is 8.9% with A01, 6.1% with H02 week and 21% with F08, so it is not
  a copy of the reversal or volatility readings; it just does not carry an edge on its own.
- **Stale symbols in the raw log.** SKSMICRO (13% of the raw bullish top five) and ABIRLANUVO (13% of the raw bearish)
  keep scoring years after their rename or merger; the engine's universe filter keeps them out of the results, and the
  overlaps above drop them. Logged in `state/engine-issues.md` (WATCH, stale rows).
