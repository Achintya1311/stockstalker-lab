# A05B: MAX effect, cross-sectionally ranked (A05 v2): diagnosis

Train split (2015-01-01 to 2020-12-31), week horizon, `top` selection (5 picks/table/date) unless noted.
Source replay: `experiments/done/a05b-max-effect-ranked-week/` (7b402dd). Card: `cards/A05B.yml`.

## What the card tests

A05 v1 never fired bullish because `-MAX/sigma` was almost never positive (`research/diagnostics/a05.md`).
A05B keeps v1's per-symbol `z = MAX / sigma` and ranks it across stocks each date (mid-rank percentile,
`score = 1 - 2 * pct`), as Bali, Cakici and Whitelaw (2011) do. The new information is the bullish side:
do the least lottery-like stocks (the most muted best day relative to their own volatility) lead over
the next week?

Bullish per-year edge: -0.26, +5.80, +0.20, +3.95, +1.75, +1.05 (2015-2020). Mostly positive, but small
and uneven, with two years (2016, 2018) carrying most of it. Bearish is identical to A05 v1's bearish,
pick for pick: ranking is a monotone transform of v1's own z, so the five highest-z stocks are the same
on all 295 dates. That identity is what crashed `evidence.yml` (fixed in d68333e). The `all` selection
(about 20k picks a side) is flat on both sides (bullish +0.32, t 1.26; bearish +0.39, t 1.57), so
whatever the bullish `top` picks have is concentrated in the extreme tail, not spread across the
bottom half of the ranking.

## Week/bullish context

Against the last evidence report's week/bullish cell (21 experiments, SPA p=0.769, StepM empty),
A05B's +2.23 would sit first, ahead of A04 (+1.92), O05 (+1.90) and A06 low volatility (+1.55). That is
the same "best of a flat cohort" picture A04's diagnosis described, not a standout: t=1.86 is short of
gate 2 (t >= 3), and the cell as a whole clears nothing once snooping is counted. The next evidence run
(re-triggered by d68333e) will place it properly.

Approximate daily top-5 overlap (Jaccard, before the universe filter) with A06 low volatility's bullish
picks is 46%, and with A04's is 0.3%. So A05B-bullish is half a low-volatility tilt: a stock whose best
day is small relative to its own volatility tends to be a quiet stock. Not an independent signal from
A06; close to independent from A04.

## Does not qualify as a go-live P1 candidate

SPEC.md section 10's provisional rule needs at least 500 train picks, a positive train edge and a
positive train directional move on a table. Bullish has the picks and the edge but loses after costs
(-0.50%); bearish fails both legs. Same shape as A04-bullish and most of the week-bullish cell:
a hit-rate edge that does not survive costs.

## What to try next

- Not worth tuning `max_window`/`sigma_window` on train: t=1.86 inside a cohort whose SPA p is 0.77 is
  where a grid search finds noise.
- If the week-bullish table stays provisional, A05B, A06 and A04 bullish are the three best week-bullish
  readings and come from two families (lottery/low-vol and 52-week range). They are natural inputs for
  P2's `consensus_v1` rank-average, which asks whether weak, unrelated signals add up.
- Retire A05 v1's bearish as a separate idea: A05B carries it unchanged.
