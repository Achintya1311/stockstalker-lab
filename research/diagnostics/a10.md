## Pre-declared rule

Predicted wrong-way (about -1 to -3) on low coverage. **Held in sign, and stronger than predicted**: -5.41,
negative in every year, one of the lowest day-bullish readings in the lab. Helps (+2.0 with interval above 0):
**not met**. The catalog row's question ("whichever direction holds on NSE is the finding") has its answer: on
NSE F&O stocks a strong-volume gap up through resistance fades the next day; it does not go.

## Where it works and fails

- **It is A09's bearish side with two breakout filters.** Approximate top-5 overlap (no universe filter): 57% of
  A10's picks are in A09's day-bearish top five, 21% in E2A's (the day-bearish draft), 0% in T04's bullish.
- **The breakout filters pick the gaps that fade hardest.** Mirror arithmetic: gross move -0.81% + 0.415% delivery
  cost = -0.40%; called bearish, about +0.40% - 0.25% futures cost = +0.15% after costs, with an edge near +5 on
  the bearish line, against A09 bearish's +3.21 and +0.03%. Volume and a close above resistance make a gap-up
  fade stronger, not weaker.
- No year is close to zero (-3.9 to -7.8), so this is not one bad period.
