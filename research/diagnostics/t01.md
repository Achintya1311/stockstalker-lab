## Why it is not a P1 candidate

Both bearish cells fail the directional-move leg (-0.06% day, -0.03% week). The picks fall more often
than the market line predicts, but not by enough on average to clear costs. This is the same shape as
the day-bearish fade family (A02, T13, T06): hit-rate edge without money in it.

## Overlap

T01 and T15 (ADX regime) pick about 90% of the same week-bearish stocks each date (day: 89%), so they are
one trend family, not two signals. T01's week-bearish overlaps A01 by about 6% and D02-contrarian by about
1%: close to independent of both.

## What to try next

- As a **P2 consensus** member for week-bearish: one of {T01, T15} (not both), with D02-contrarian and
  A01, which are near-independent of it and of each other. The consensus may lift the directional move
  that none of the trend picks reaches alone.
- As a **filter**, the same way E2A filtered A02: keep T01's bearish picks only where another condition
  (OI rising, extreme-low PCR) agrees. A confluence card, pre-declared.
- Not worth tuning windows: the hit-rate edge is already stable; the gap is magnitude, not sign.
