## Where it works and fails

- **Only the bullish side works.** A narrow-range day inside an uptrend (20-day SMA above 50-day) is
  followed by more rising days than the universe, in every year for NR7. The mirror inside a downtrend has
  no edge (NR7 bearish negative in five of six years). So this is not "range contraction precedes a move in
  the trend's direction" in general, only on the upside.
- **The stricter window is stronger.** NR7 (narrowest of 7 days) beats NR4 (narrowest of 4) on edge and
  steadiness, the direction Crabel's claim predicts. The two share 64% of NR7's top-5 picks, so they are
  largely one reading, and the evidence report counts both as separate trials.
- **Uneven by year:** most of NR7 bullish's edge is from 2017-2018 (+4.64, +4.24); the other four years are
  +0.6 to +1.2.
- **It does not pay the delivery cost either.** Directional move after costs is -0.49%, so the picks' gross
  open-to-close move averages about -0.07%: they rise more often than the universe, but the average move is
  slightly negative. Same pattern as T04 (`t04.md`).
- **Independent of the other StepM day-bullish readings:** share of NR7's top-5 picks also in D01C's 1.1%,
  in T04's 1.5%. It overlaps more with T11 inside bar (34%), which is itself flat (-0.37).
