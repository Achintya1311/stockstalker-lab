## Where it fails

Day side fails almost every year 2015-2019, with 2020 the only (mild) bullish exception (+0.71). Week
side has no clean pattern — it swings between strongly negative (2018 bullish -6.24, 2015 bearish -7.56)
and moderately positive years with no obvious regime driving the sign flip from year-level numbers alone.

## What this means

As a placebo with no impulse/displacement requirement, O00 is exactly what SPEC.md section 5 calls "no
claim is assumed true" made concrete: it has no real hypothesis behind it (any last-opposite-colour
candle, not a genuine order block), and the data agrees — it loses to the market line everywhere, as a
placebo should. Its only job is to be beaten by O01; see `o01.md`.
