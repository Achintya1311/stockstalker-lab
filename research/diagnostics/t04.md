## Where it works and fails

- **Bullish is the most consistent bullish reading in the lab:** positive in every train year, interval
  well above zero, and one of three day-bullish readings StepM names as individually superior (with D01C
  and T10-NR7; SPA p=0.003). It overlaps little with them (share of its top-5 picks also in D01C's 11.0%,
  T10-NR7's 2.2%, T06's 12.8%, T13's 11.6%).
- **But the bullish picks do not move enough to pay the delivery cost.** Directional move after costs is
  -0.415%, which is the whole 0.415% delivery cost: the gross open-to-close move of the picks averages about
  zero. They rise more often than the universe (48.6% vs a 45.2% market line), but the rises are small. So
  under SPEC section 10's letter T04 bullish is not eligible, the same reason no bullish reading is.
- **Bearish narrowly misses on cost:** gross move about +0.24% against a 0.25% futures cost, so -0.012%
  after costs. The edge is weaker than the eligible day-bearish readings (E2A +7.46, D01C +4.74) and 2020
  was negative.
- Coverage is about three dates in four: the conjunction of trend filter and RSI extreme does not fire
  every day.
