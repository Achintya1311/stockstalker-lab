# O05 (order block on weekly-resampled bars) vs O01 alone: train diagnosis

`state/queue.md`'s order-block lane: "once O05's train replay lands, diagnose it against O01's own week
cells." O05's replay landed this run (`experiments/done/o05-order-block-weekly-week/`). Sources: that
`results.md`/`results.json`, `experiments/done/o01-retest-week/results.md`, and a direct week-by-week
comparison of `picks.json` for O01 (week) and O05 (both `top`/5-pick selection, train split).

This is a horizon-mechanics check, not a new confirming signal (unlike O02-O04): same detector
(`strategies/ob_core.py`), same `DEFAULTS`, run on weekly-resampled OHLC instead of daily OHLC. The
question is whether the zone-and-retest idea holds up, or holds up *better*, at a slower timeframe.

## O05 vs O01 alone (top / 5-pick selection, train)

| Side | O01 week edge (t) | O05 week edge (t) | Delta | O01 directional move | O05 directional move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bullish | -2.21 (-1.86) | **+1.90 (1.47)** | **+4.11 (reverses sign)** | -0.50 | -0.39 |
| Bearish | +0.18 (0.12) | +0.32 (0.21) | +0.14 (flat, still insignificant) | -0.11 | **-0.31 (worse)** |

**Bullish is the real finding here.** O01's week-bullish was clearly net-negative (t = -1.86, only 1 of 6
years positive, per `o01.md`/`o03.md`) — the worst cell in the whole ablation on a plain significance
read. Resampling the same detector to weekly bars flips the sign to positive (t = 1.47, 4 of 5 covered
years positive) — still well short of gate 2's t ≥ 3 and not yet a confirmed edge, but this is the first
time in the O00→O01→O02→O03→O04→O05 sequence that the *bullish* side has shown a positive edge at all.
The `all` selection shows the same direction (O01 all-bullish -1.77 (t -1.78) → O05 all-bullish +0.90 (t
0.81)), so this isn't a top-5 selection artefact.

**Bearish stays where it was** — small and insignificant on both bar sizes — and its after-cost
directional move gets worse on weekly bars (-0.11 → -0.31), the opposite of the bullish side's small
improvement (-0.50 → -0.39). Weekly resampling is not a uniform improvement; it helps the side that was
failing and mildly hurts the side that was already closest to even.

## How much is this just a different read, vs the same picks seen from further away?

Week-by-week top-5 overlap on the 254 dates both strategies cover (`picks.json`, `top` selection,
Jaccard on the 5-symbol sets):

| Side | Common dates | Avg. overlap (Jaccard) | Weeks with identical top-5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bullish | 254 | **2.4%** | 0.0% |
| Bearish | 254 | **1.6%** | 0.0% |

This is categorically different from O02/O03's finding ("mostly a subset of O01's own picks", 69-79%
overlap) and from O04's finding (44-45% bullish / 73-74% bearish, a re-ranking of the *same* pool). O05's
top-5 lists are almost completely disjoint from O01's, week to week, on both sides. That is expected
mechanically — a zone built from one week's aggregate OHLC range and a zone built from five separate
daily ranges are different objects, found on different symbols, even with the identical detector code —
but it means O05 is not a confirmation or a filter of O01 at all. It is a structurally independent read
of the same underlying hypothesis (price returning to an impulsive origin), which is exactly why its
bullish sign can flip while using unchanged detector logic: it isn't disagreeing with O01 on the same
candidates, it's looking at an almost entirely different candidate set.

## Coverage note

O05 only starts 2016-02-22 (254 weeks) vs O01's 2015-03-30 (301 weeks): the card's `MIN_WEEKS=60` floor
(`cards/O05.yml`) burns roughly a year of weekly bars before the detector has enough weekly history to
fire, on top of the daily `MIN_ROWS=60` floor O01 also has. The by-year tables in `results.md` reflect
this — O05 has no 2015 row at all — so the whole-split edge numbers above aren't on identical calendars;
the week-by-week overlap comparison above is, since it only uses the 254 dates both logs share.

## Where O05-bullish works and fails, by year

Per `results.json` (`top` selection, bullish): 2016 +2.32, 2017 +1.64, 2018 +2.44, 2019 +3.48, 2020
-0.33. Four of five years positive and reasonably stable in size (2.3-3.5 points) other than a flat 2020
— a much steadier year-by-year pattern than O01's own week-bullish (`o01.md`: -5.36 in 2016, -4.84 in
2020, only 2015 positive). The 2020 dip lines up with every other order-block cell's own weakest years in
this lane (O03's diagnosis flags 2020 pressure too), consistent with a regime where the broader market's
own volatility swamps a structural retest signal rather than something specific to weekly bars.

## What this means for "do order blocks help?"

**Still not shown, but this is the first genuinely encouraging number in the lane.** O05's bullish edge
is the only cell across six ablation steps (O00, O01, O02, O03, O04, O05) that is both positive *and*
reasonably stable across years — O01's own positive cells (day-bearish, week-bearish) were small,
inconsistent year to year, and got worse under every confirmation attempt (O02, O03, O04). O05's
week-bullish is t = 1.47, not t ≥ 3, so it is not a gate pass and not close to one; it is a reason to keep
looking at weekly-structure order blocks specifically, not a result.

**Do order blocks help? (running answer, train only):** On the bearish side, still no — O01's one lead
there has been weakened by every confirmation attempt (O02 kept it day-only, O03 flipped week-bearish
negative, O04 flipped both bearish cells negative, O05 leaves it flat). On the bullish side, the answer
moves from a firm "no" (O00-O04 all negative or near-zero) to "not yet, but weekly-bar structure is the
first candidate worth a second look" — contingent on this being confirmed independently, since O05's
near-zero overlap with O01 means it hasn't been cross-checked against anything else yet.
