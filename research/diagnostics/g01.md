## Where it works and fails

- **The highest week-bearish train edge in the lab** (53 week-bearish readings; A01 is +2.48, T05C +4.02), positive in
  every year, and 2019 (+9.69) is the only outsized one. Without 2019 it is still about +2.8.
- **It fails only on cost.** The bearish cost is about 0.25% a week (0.05% futures STT plus 10 bps slippage each way),
  so the gross mean move is about +0.20%: residual losers keep drifting down, but by less than the cost. This is the
  same wall that stopped T04, T06, T13 and Q07.
- **A different set of stocks.** Bearish top-5 overlap: 13.3% A03, 7.0% A01 (the draft), 6.4% G02, 1.1% T05C, 28.5% G03.
  Bullish: 43.8% A03, 34.9% G03, 0.6% A05B. Scaling by residual volatility is what separates it from A03 (A03
  bearish is +0.85): the losers it picks fall steadily rather than crash.
- **Bullish:** steady residual winners beat the market line in every year but by little outside 2019, and the move is
  -0.51% after the 0.415% delivery cost.
