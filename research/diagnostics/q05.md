## Where it works and fails

- **Steady fallers keep drifting down for a week, by a little.** Five of six years positive, the most
  consistent week-bearish reading among the Q cards. The gross move is about +0.12% in the picked direction
  (-0.13% + 0.25% futures cost), half what the 0.25% cost needs.
- **It is a different set of stocks from the week-bearish draft.** Approximate top-5 overlap (no universe
  filter): 0.1% with A01 (which fades last week's winners, the opposite kind of stock), 14% with I01, 12% with
  T01, 12% with T15, and 31% with Q03. It belongs to the slow-trend persistence family (T01, T15, I01, Q03),
  which has the same shape everywhere: hit rate above the line, gross move too small to pay the cost.
- **Smooth rises do not persist.** Bullish swings between +3.45 and -4.79 by year. The lab's bullish
  continuation readings keep failing in the same way (T08, T09, T16, T17, T21).
