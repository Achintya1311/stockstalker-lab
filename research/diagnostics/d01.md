# D01 (OI build-up alone): train diagnosis

`strategies/d01_oi_buildup.py`, day horizon only. Source: `experiments/done/d01-oi-buildup-day/results.md`.
No diagnosis existed for this card despite a finished replay since 2026-10-22 — written now because the
order-block lane's O02 comparison needs it as the baseline.

## Headline (top / 5-pick selection)

| Side | Picks | Coverage | Accuracy | Market line | Edge | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bullish | 7304 | 99.86% | 40.32% | 44.93% | **-4.61** | -8.15 |
| Bearish | 7334 | 100.00% | 52.06% | 54.54% | **-2.48** | -4.59 |

Both sides lose to the market line, confidently (t well past the gate's significance bar, but in the
wrong direction — this is a confidently *wrong* signal, not noise). 0% of years positive on either side
(all six train years negative bullish; all six negative bearish too). Full un-selected (`all`) numbers
agree in sign and magnitude, so this isn't a top-5-selection artefact.

## Where it fails

- **Every year, both sides.** Not a regime-specific failure — 2015 through 2020 are all negative, with no
  recovery even in a single year. Bearish is closest to flat in 2020 (-0.19) but still negative.
- **Bullish is the worse side** (-4.61 vs -2.48), and gets worse as the picked set is restricted to the
  top 5 (`top` -4.61 vs `all` -1.72) — the strategy's most-confident long build-up picks are its worst
  picks, the opposite of what a real signal should do.

## What this means for the OI build-up hypothesis

The desk convention this card tests — "price up + OI up = long build-up, read as bullish continuation;
price down + OI up = short build-up, read as bearish continuation" — does not hold on train, on either
side, in any year. If anything the sign looks backwards on the bullish side (its most-confident picks
underperform the market line the most). This matches a crowded-trade / already-priced-in story: by the
time build-up is visible in the data a strategy can see (yesterday's close), the move it would "confirm"
has often already happened or is about to mean-revert.

## What to try next

- Don't trust this card's sign as stated. If anyone re-tests it, try the **contrarian** read (long
  build-up → fade, not continue) before assuming the convention itself is simply wrong — the catalog's
  own prior_claim calls it a "standard desk reading, untested here," and standard readings sold on
  trading desks are exactly the kind of claim this lab exists to check, not assume.
- This is the baseline O02 gates against. O02's bearish side (see `research/diagnostics/o02.md`) turns
  D01's confidently-wrong bearish edge (-2.48) into roughly flat (+0.01) by requiring an order-block
  retest to agree — i.e. combining with O01 removes the harm without adding real edge. That is itself
  informative: D01 alone is actively harmful, and OB-gating is a (partial, low-coverage) fix for that
  harm rather than a source of new edge.
