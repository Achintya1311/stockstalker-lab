## Pre-declared rule

Helps if a side's top-5 edge is at least +2.0, with at least 4 positive years and a positive directional move
after costs. **Not met on either side**: bullish +1.85 with 3 positive years; bearish +1.58 with a directional
move of -0.02%.

## Where it works and fails

- **The level carries a small, broad signal on both sides.** In the whole universe, calmer-than-median stocks
  beat the market line by about 0.9 points a week and more-volatile ones lose by about 1.0, both with t near
  2.4. The top five are not sharper than that, only noisier.
- **The prediction for the calm side held:** positive edge, but the five calmest stocks move little. The gross
  weekly move is about +0.09% against the 0.415% delivery cost.
- **The volatile side nearly pays its cost.** The five most volatile stocks' gross bearish move is about
  +0.23% a week against the 0.25% futures cost (-0.02% after it). Eight week-bearish readings in the lab are
  above it on this measure, led by T05C (+0.24%) and T05 (+0.23%). 2017 is the only losing year (-3.03).
- **New picks, not a copy:** 0.3% of the bullish top five are A06's (A06 scores the change in a stock's own
  volatility) and 0.3% champion v1's. On the bearish side, 13% overlap with A06, 14% with T05C, 4% with A01.
