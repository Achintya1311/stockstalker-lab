  with A03, 7% with A01, 2% with T05C. So I01's bearish side is mostly different stocks from the week-bearish
  draft choice.
- **Costs decide it.** Bullish gross move about -0.21% a week, so even the right direction would not pay the
  0.415% delivery cost.
