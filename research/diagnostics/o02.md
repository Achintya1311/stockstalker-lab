`experiments/done/d01-oi-buildup-day/results.md`, and `research/diagnostics/d01.md` for D01's own standalone
diagnosis.

## Reading it against the lab's own bar

**Mixed, split exactly by side** — the same split O01-vs-O00 showed (`o01.md`):

- **Bearish: O02 beats D01, clearly.** D01 alone is confidently wrong on the bearish side (-2.48, t
  -4.59 — the kind of number that would need explaining if it were edge, not harm). Gating it by the OB
  retest agreeing brings it to essentially flat (+0.01, t 0.01): the combination doesn't add edge, but it
  removes almost all of D01's harm on this side. That is a real, useful finding even though it is not
  itself a positive edge.
- **Bullish: O02 is worse than D01, and D01 was already bad.** -5.15 vs -4.61. Narrowing the OI-alone
  picks down to the 81% that also pass an order-block retest makes the bullish side *more* wrong, not
  less. The lower t-stat (-4.60 vs -8.15) is just fewer picks (2421 vs 7304), not less confidence per pick
  — accuracy itself drops further (39.98% vs 40.32%).

## Where it fails

- **By year, bullish:** 2015 is the one positive year (+6.62) and it is a big outlier in the other
  direction too — 2016 is -11.73, the single worst number anywhere in the O00-O02/D01 set. 2017-2020 are
  all moderately negative (-4.8 to -6.4). One good year out of six (16.67% positive years) is not a
  pattern to trust.
- **By year, bearish — the cell that "works":** the improvement over D01 is not uniform across years, it is
  a genuine trend. 2015 is still bad (-5.63); 2016-2017 are near flat (-1.26, -0.59); **2018, 2019, 2020 are
  all positive and increasing (+0.50, +1.12, +4.05)**. Three of six years positive (50%), versus D01
  bearish's 0% positive years — the gate is doing real year-over-year work, concentrated in the second
  half of train.
- **Coverage drops hard on both sides** (bullish 99.86% -> 81.14%, bearish 100% -> 72.71%), as expected
  from stacking two independent abstention conditions (needs both an unmitigated order-block retest *and*
  D01's build-up classification to agree in direction). The 2015-2016 weakness could plausibly be a
  coverage/data-maturity artefact rather than a regime effect: F&O had far fewer FUTSTK names and thinner
  OI reporting in the earliest train years, so the near-month OI change `oi_chg_futstk` D01 depends on may
  simply be noisier before the market matured — worth checking against `state/data_manifest.json`'s
  per-year F&O universe size before reading 2015-2016 as a real "regime" rather than a data artefact.
- **Sector / weekday breakdown:** same limitation as `o01.md` — not available from `picks.json` alone in
  this sandbox; would need an engine-side cut.

## What this means for "do order blocks help?"

Taken with `o01.md`: **neither ablation step (requiring an impulse, then requiring OI agreement on top)
produces a strategy with real positive train edge on either side.** What both steps consistently do is
the same thing — narrow a confidently-wrong bearish signal (O00's placebo, then D01 alone) toward flat,
at a real coverage cost, while leaving or worsening the bullish side. That is a genuine, repeated pattern
across two independent gates (impulse-only, then impulse+OI), which makes it more likely to be real than
either single result alone — but "turns losing bets into coin flips" is a materially weaker claim than
"order blocks help," and it is specifically a bearish-side, not a bullish-side, effect so far.

## What to try next

- **O03 (OB gated by T01 trend)** is next in the queue regardless; worth specifically watching whether it
  repeats this same bearish-helps/bullish-hurts split, which would strengthen the "there's something real
  about bearish order-block retests, nothing about bullish ones, on this data" reading, or break the
  pattern, which would suggest O01/O02's bearish improvement is itself not robust.
- **Before trusting the bearish 2018-2020 trend as real edge rather than data maturity**, a later `diagnose`
  unit should split D01 and O02 by early (2015-2017) vs late (2018-2020) train years explicitly and check
  F&O universe size / OI data completeness per `state/data_manifest.json` for those years.
- **Coverage cost is real.** At 72-81% coverage, O02 already abstains on roughly 1 in 4-5 dates for the
  5-pick table; a four-way AND (adding trend in O03, or rank-averaging in O04) will cut coverage further,
  and SPEC.md section 3 requires coverage be reported next to accuracy for any selective strategy — keep
  an eye on whether later ablation steps abstain their way to a thin, cherry-picked sample rather than a
  genuinely better one.
