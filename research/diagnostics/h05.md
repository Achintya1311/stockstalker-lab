## Where it works and fails

- **Zero on both sides.** Neither edge is distinguishable from zero; the bullish side swings with the market's own
  years (2016 and 2020 crashes and rebounds at -4, 2017-2019 at +2 to +4), which is momentum-crash behaviour, not a
  stock-specific effect.
- **Mostly A03 again.** Half the bearish top five and two-fifths of the bullish top five are A03's picks, and A03
  itself is +0.21 / +0.85. Skipping the latest six months does not find the edge that 12-1 momentum lacks.
- One-week holding on large caps is far from the paper's monthly, small-stock setting (card red flag 2).
