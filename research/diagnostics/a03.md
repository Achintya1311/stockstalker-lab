# A03: price momentum 12-1 (Jegadeesh & Titman 1993) — diagnosis

Train split (2015-01-01 to 2020-12-31), week horizon, `top` selection (5 picks/table/date) unless noted.
Source replay: `experiments/done/a03-momentum-12-1-week/`. Joint test: `reports/evidence_train.md`
(week/bullish, week/bearish cells).

## What the card tests

Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) rank US stocks by trailing 12-month return, skip the most recent month, and
find winners keep winning / losers keep losing over the following 3-12 months. The paper's own horizon
is months; `cards/A03.yml` deliberately tests whether any of that continuation shows up at NSE's
one-week horizon instead — a published 3-12-month finding tested far outside its own horizon, flagged
as a red flag on the card itself. Score is signed continuous 12-1 trailing return: biggest past winners
score bullish, biggest past losers score bearish (opposite sign convention from A01's short-term
reversal, which fades the same kind of past return instead of continuing it).

Both sides are indistinguishable from zero (|t| < 1 on both) and both have a negative after-cost
directional move — the opposite of a usable edge even before the significance question. Per-year edge
swings sign repeatedly on both sides (bullish: -5.46, +0.20, +1.68, +5.21, -1.26; bearish: -0.03, -2.26,
+1.85, +6.61, -2.05) with no visible trend — this reads as noise around zero, not a weak-but-real signal
obscured by a couple of bad years. Only 5 covered years (2016-2020): the 252-day lookback plus 21-day
skip consumes 2015 entirely, so there is less data here than most other week-horizon cards.

The joint test confirms this is unremarkable within its own cohort, not just absolutely: on week/bearish
(23 experiments, SPA p=0.190, StepM names zero strategies) A03 ranks 8th of 23 at a marginal +0.85 edge,
well behind the cell's actual candidates (d02-stock-pcr-contrarian +3.75, t01-sma-crossover +3.20,
t15-adx-regime +2.95, a01-weekly-reversal +2.48). On week/bullish (21 experiments, SPA p=0.769 — the
single weakest cell of all four) A03's +0.21 sits in the middle of a field that itself clears nothing.

## Does not qualify as a go-live P1 candidate

SPEC.md section 10's provisional rule needs ≥500 train picks (A03 has that on both sides), a positive
train edge (technically true on both sides here, but only barely — +0.21 and +0.85 are well inside
noise) and a positive train directional move for that side. A03 fails the third test on both sides
(-0.57%, -0.26%): whatever the hit-rate edge, picks-then-weighted-by-move loses money after costs on
both legs. Not a candidate.

## A03 vs A01: same raw ingredient, opposite sign, opposite result

A01 (Lehmann/Jegadeesh short-term reversal — fade the trailing *week's* return) and A03 (continue the
trailing *year's* return, skipping the most recent month) both rank stocks by a past-return window and
bet on the sign, just at opposite horizons and opposite directions. A01-bearish cleared all three
go-live provisional bars (edge +2.48, directional move +0.02%) even though its own joint-test footing is
shaky (same week/bearish cell, SPA not significant, StepM empty). A03 clears none. The two readings
together say: on NSE at a one-week horizon, a *very recent* (trailing-week) return has some fade
tendency worth continuing to watch, but a *stale* (trailing-year, month-skipped) return carries no
detectable continuation at all — the publication's own 3-12-month horizon claim does not telescope down
to one week. That is a clean, specific negative, not just "didn't reach significance."

## What to try next

- The published effect is 3-12 months. If this family is tested again, test it at its own horizon (a
  monthly-rebalance or multi-week pick) rather than one week — this card's one-week test is a genuine
  stress test of the transfer, not a fair replication, and a null here says nothing about the paper's
  own claim.
- Not worth tuning `lookback_days`/`skip_days` on train: the edge is flat-to-negative across all 5 years
  with no stable plateau to find — a grid search here would just be snooping on noise.
- No confluence variant is proposed: unlike the day-bearish fade family (A02/A08/A09), there is no
  positive base edge here for a filter to sharpen.
