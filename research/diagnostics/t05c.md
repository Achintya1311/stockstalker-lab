## What this does and does not show

- It is the mirror of T05's bullish loss, so the edge is the same evidence seen from the other side, not
  new evidence. What the replay adds is the cost question: called bearish, the up-cross stocks are costed
  as a futures trade, and on the week the move is large enough to clear it.
- **Joint test:** in week/bearish (28 experiments) T05C is the only reading StepM names as individually
  superior, and the cell's SPA p-value drops from 0.202 to 0.099 with it included. It is the first
  week-bearish reading to survive any snooping control. In day/bearish StepM names it too (SPA p=0.000),
  but day-bearish already has stronger readings (E2A +7.46, D01C +4.74, the day-bearish consensus +4.61, A09 +3.21).
- **A researcher degree of freedom:** the flip was chosen after T05's train result. That is why it is a
  logged trial with its rule written in advance, and why only validation can confirm it.
- **Independent of the other week-bearish readings:** share of its top-5 picks also in A01's 7.0%, T15's
  7.5%, D02-contrarian's 0.9%, the week-bearish consensus's 10.2%, T05's 0% (disjoint by construction).
  So "stocks that just had a fresh MACD up-cross" are not simply A01's biggest weekly gainers.
- 2015 is the exception on both horizons, and the week interval's lower end (+0.79) is close to zero.
