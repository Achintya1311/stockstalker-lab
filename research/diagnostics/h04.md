Card: `cards/H04.yml`. Rule: for the month of the coming Monday, the stock's mean month-end-to-month-end return in that
calendar month over every earlier complete year (at least two); highest bullish, lowest bearish.

Predicted both sides -1.5 to +1.5 (held: -1.24, -0.03), both directional moves negative (held), and an expected failure

## Where it works and fails

- **2017 only.** Both sides were positive in 2017, the first year with two prior years of history, then 2018 reversed
  both. With two to five earlier observations per calendar month the score is mostly last year's month for that stock,
  plus the market's own month, as red flag 2 warned.
- **Independent of the rest.** Top-5 overlap is 6.8% with H05's bullish picks and 5.2% with A01's bearish picks, so it is
  not a disguised momentum or reversal reading; it is simply noise at this history length.
