## Where it works and fails

- **Bearish divergence is the steadier side**: positive in every year after 2015, but small. Its gross move is
  about +0.12% a week, half the 0.25% futures cost.
- **Close to independent of the lab's week-bearish choices:** 4.7% of its top five are in A01's, 4.5% in
  A01_I01F's and 1.3% in T05C's. A new high on falling volume is a different stock from last week's biggest
  winner.
- **Bullish divergence is a stock at a new 20-day low**, the oversold group that lags in T03 and T12.
