## Where it works and fails

- **Breakouts are followed by underperformance the next week, on both sides.** The bullish breakout is
  significantly wrong-way in both windows, and the longer window is worse (-5.09 against -3.97), the opposite
  of what a trend-following claim predicts. The bearish breakdown also loses (-2.72 for 20 days), so a stock
  at a new extreme tends to give some of it back, whichever direction.
- **Not a ranking effect:** the `all` selection is as bad (20-day bullish -3.54, t -3.33), so every breakout is
  wrong-way, not just the largest. This differs from T09 and champion v1, where only the top five were.
- **Unstable by year:** 2016 and 2020 carry most of the bullish loss (-11.25 and -8.76 for 20 days).
- Shares 36% of its week-bullish top five with T09's (`t09.md`): the "most extended" family again.
