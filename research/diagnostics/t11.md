## Where it works and fails

- **No edge on either side, in either selection.** Every interval straddles zero, and the per-year values
  are small and change sign.
- **The inside bar removes what the trend filter had.** T01's 20/50 reading on its own is +1.89 day-bearish
  (t 3.30, 6/6 years) and -1.46 day-bullish. Keeping only inside-bar days takes the bearish side to -0.08, so
  whatever carries T01's bearish edge is not on the quiet days.
- Both directional moves after costs are negative. Bearish has the smallest gap (gross about +0.14% against
  the 0.25% futures cost), but with no edge to go with it.
