## Pre-declared rule

Predicted day bearish small positive and day bullish within noise. Bearish held (+1.67). **Bullish did not**: it
is the stronger side, positive in every year. Helps (+2.0, 4 of 6 years, positive directional move): **not met**
on either side, on the directional move alone. Bullish edge and years clear the bar; its gross move is about
-0.01% (-0.42% + 0.415% delivery cost), so it is right more often than the market line but by tiny moves.
Bearish gross move is about +0.20% against the 0.25% futures cost.

## Where it works and fails

- **The wick is new information.** Approximate top-5 overlap with every other day reading is 0.3% to 2.8%
  (no universe filter): bullish 1.1% with T04, 2.5% with T10-NR7, 2.8% with T25, 2.0% with D01C, 0.7% with A02;
  bearish 0.3% with E2A, 0.4% with A02, 2.7% with D01C, 1.5% with A09, 1.1% with T06. None of the lab's
  reversal, trend or open-interest readings picks these stocks.
- **Weakest in 2018 on bullish (+0.63)**, otherwise +1.7 to +4.6; no sign of decay toward 2020 large enough to
  call a trend.
- **Ties are not driving the top five.** A score of exactly 1 (no upper wick, the whole range below the body)
  appears on about 0.35 symbols per date, and the 5th and 6th scores tie on 19 of 1,484 dates, so the
  alphabetical tie-break that broke the calendar cards does not apply here.
- The red flag holds in part: a tiny-range bar scores the same as a wide one. Restricting to bars whose range is
  large relative to the stock's own recent range is the obvious refinement, but it is a new trial.
