## Where it works and fails

- **The bearish side is wrong-way in 2016-2019** and right in 2015 and 2020. Its picks are the most liquid large caps;
  2018-19 were the years when a few large caps rose while mid caps fell, the same window that drove H06's and G02's
  wrong-way readings. This is a size-regime bet, not an illiquidity premium.
- **Not a volatility proxy here.** Only 8.3% of the bullish top five are H02's high-ivol bearish picks (8.0% the other
  way), 4.2% of the bearish top five are A06's low-volatility bullish picks, 6.5% of the bullish top five are A05B's.
  Within the F&O universe the ratio ranks mostly by traded value (size), as the card's second red flag warned.
- **No flip.** Calling the most liquid names bullish would ride the same 2018-19 regime: 2 of 6 years would turn
  negative and the 0.415% delivery cost sinks the bullish directional move in any case.
