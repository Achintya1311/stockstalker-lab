## Where it works and fails

- **Steadier than F11, not stronger.** The two extra factors lift the weak F11 years (2015 +2.5, 2017 +2.7) and give back
  the strong ones (2016 -2.1, 2019 -1.8). The lowest year is +4.10 against F11's +2.81. The 2017-18 weakness shared by
  F11, H01, F12 and F08 is not in F13: 2017 is its best year.
- **Better moves, same hit rate.** The directional move after costs doubles (+0.094% vs +0.047%) at nearly the same
  edge: the accelerating, heavy-turnover tops fall further when they fall. It is the highest dir of any full-coverage
  day-bearish reading below E2A (+0.13%; E2A_F11_FB +0.06%, D01C +0.08%).
- **Half F11, a third E2A.** Top-5 bearish overlap: F11 50%, A02 41%, F10 36%, E2A 36% (on E2A's dates), D01C 22%, F07
  17%. Bullish overlap is 29% with F11, 5% with T04 and 1% with Q06: the bullish side is not an oversold reading.
- **Joint test.** StepM names it individually superior on day bearish; it ranks sixth of 66 by edge, first among the
  single (non-blend) price rules.
