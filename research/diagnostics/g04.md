## Where it works and fails

- **Not A05's MAX.** Top-5 bullish overlap 3.6% with A05B and 0.0% with A05: on 20 daily returns, the most negative
  skew comes from one large down day, so the bullish side picks recent crashes, which kept falling.
- The bearish side (one large up day, lottery-like) is steady across years but small: gross about -0.08% a week
  before costs.
