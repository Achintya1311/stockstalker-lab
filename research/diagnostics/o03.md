## Does the trend gate help, hurt, or just subset O01's picks?

**It does not repeat O01→O02's clean bearish-helps/bullish-hurts split, and the trend gate is mostly
re-ranking inside O01's own candidate pool rather than acting as an independent filter.**

- **The pattern breaks on week-bearish.** O01→O02 (OI confirmation) and now O01→O03 (trend confirmation)
  both helped day-bearish, but O02 had no week variant to check. O03 does, and it is the one cell where
  adding a gate makes O01 *worse*, not better: +0.18 (barely positive, t near zero) becomes -0.91. That
  weakens the "bearish order-block retests have something real going for them" reading from `o01.md`/
  `o02.md` — a trend filter that should, if the underlying idea is directional and genuine, help a
  bearish signal at least as much as it helped the day horizon, instead flips it negative.
- **Every other cell moves in the direction O01's own numbers already pointed, by a small amount.**
  Day-bullish and week-bullish both stay firmly negative, just less so (+0.11, +0.76); day-bearish
  stays the best cell and improves slightly (+0.23, t still under the lab's bar). None of the four
  deltas is large enough, on its own single-trial basis, to call "the trend gate helps" — three small
  positive nudges and one real negative one is not a signal, it's noise around O01's existing shape.
- **Pick overlap confirms the near-subset risk the O03 card itself flagged.** Approximating the published
  table as the daily top-5 by \|score\| from each strategy's own `picks.json` (no universe/liquidity
  restriction — not available outside the Actions cache, so this is directional, not exact), the share of
  O03's daily top-5 that O01 would also have picked that day:

  | Horizon | Side | O03 dates with picks | Share of O03's top-5 also in O01's top-5 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Day | Bullish | 1,426 of 1,426 common dates | 69.9% |
  | Day | Bearish | 1,349 of 1,413 O01 dates | 78.6% |
  | Week | Bullish | 301 of 301 common dates | 69.2% |
  | Week | Bearish | 285 of 298 O01 dates | 78.0% |

  70-79% overlap is high: on most dates, three or four of O03's five picks are stocks O01 would have
  surfaced anyway, just re-ordered by whichever also clears the trend filter. O03 is not finding a
  materially different set of stocks — it is **mostly subsetting and re-ranking O01's own picks**, exactly
  the risk `cards/O03.yml`'s own red flag named before this run had numbers to check it against. The small
  per-cell edge deltas above are consistent with that: a strategy that mostly re-ranks within the same
  pool should move numbers by a little, not transform them, and that's what happened on three of four
  cells.

## Where it works and fails

- **By year, day-bearish (O03's best cell, like O01's):** per `results.json`'s `by_year`, 2018 is again
  the standout (edge +3.52), consistent with O01's own best year on this cell (+3.86) — the trend gate
  keeps most of 2018's signal rather than diluting it. 2015-2017 and 2019-2020 are smaller and mixed,
  same shape as O01.
- **By year, week-bearish (O03's worst relative move):** this cell's `positive_year_share` drops from
  O01's 50% to 33% — fewer years clear zero, not just a magnitude shift in the same years, which is a
  real behavioural change, not noise from one outlier year.
- **Coverage cost is the most consistent effect across all four cells**: 3.4-8.6 points of coverage lost
  to the extra AND, every time. That's the one unambiguous finding — a trend-agreement gate narrows the
  candidate pool by a meaningful, consistent amount regardless of side or horizon, exactly as a second
  independent-ish filter should.
- **Sector/weekday breakdown:** not available, same limitation `o01.md`/`o02.md` already recorded —
  `picks.json` carries no outcomes, and the real dataset never leaves the Actions cache.

## Answer to "do order blocks help?" (train only, so far)

**Still no, and O03 weakens rather than strengthens the one lead (bearish) the ablation had.** Gating O01
by T01's trend spread moves every cell a little, in the direction O01's own shape already pointed, except
week-bearish, which flips from flat-positive to clearly negative. Combined with 70-79% pick overlap with
O01's own top-5s, the most defensible read is: **O03 is close to a trend-filtered subset of O01, not an
independent confirmation, and that subset is not obviously better than O01 unfiltered.** The day-bearish
cell remains the only one with a positive, if still insignificant, edge across O00→O01→O02(no week
variant)→O03; week-bearish — previously the second-best cell — no longer supports the bearish-side
story once a trend gate is added.
