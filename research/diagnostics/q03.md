## Pre-declared rule

Predicted bullish negative on both horizons (the A01/A02 reversal) and bearish near zero. Bullish was not
wrong-way: day -0.52 and week +0.69, both noise. Bearish came out mildly positive instead. Helps (+2.0, 4 of 6

## Where it works and fails

- **Counting up and down closes carries almost nothing.** The only cell near +2 rests on one year (2017, +8.90);
  the other five years average about +0.6. The bootstrap interval includes 0 everywhere.
- **The reversal story does not carry over.** A02 and A01 fade the biggest recent moves; a high count of up days is
  a different thing (many small gains), and its top five overlap A02's day-bearish picks only 6.8%. Q03's
  day-bearish top five share 10.7% with T01's day-bearish picks, so the faint bearish lean is the trend family's
  mild persistence again, not a new effect.
- **Week bearish is 31% the same stocks as Q05 week-bearish** (steady fallers), the highest overlap among the Q
  cards; both readings point at the same slow-drift persistence. Neither clears the cost test.
- The score takes few distinct values (0.31, 0.41, 0.51, 0.61 cover most top-five picks), so the move-size
  tie-break decides much of each top five; that is by design and not a defect.
