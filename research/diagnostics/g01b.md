## Where it works and fails

- **The idea was backwards.** Two thirds of G01B's picks are G01's, so the third it swapped (calm residual losers out,
  volatile ones in) carried G01's edge. Volatile losers rebound more often and further: the hit rate fell by about 2
  points and the average move turned more against the pick, not less.
- Without 2019 (+9.69, the same year that lifts G01) the edge is about +0.5.
- The lesson for the cost wall: on the week bearish side, steadier stocks give the better hit rate, and larger movers do
  not bring larger moves in the picked direction. Lifting the gross move needs a different signal, not a volatility
  screen on a good one.
