## Where it works and fails

- **The stock-specific part is the signal.** Top-5 bearish overlap is 45.8% with A06 (total volatility), yet A06's
  bearish side is +0.15 (50% of years). Removing the market part of volatility turns a null reading into the strongest
  week-bearish one. Which of A06's picks H02 drops (likely high-beta names) is not checked yet.
- Overlap with the draft A01 is 12.0%, with F06 21.4%, T05C 2.8%, Q01C 3.0%, A05B's bearish side 22.9%: a separate
  family from the reversal readings that hold the table.
- Weakest in 2017 (+0.43), the steadiest rising year of the train split; strongest in 2015 and 2019.
- The directional move after the 0.25% futures cost is +0.13%, so the gross move is about +0.38% a week, about twice
  G01's. Wide picks: 148 distinct stocks over 1,545 bearish picks.
