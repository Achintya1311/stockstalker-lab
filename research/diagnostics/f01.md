## Where it works and fails

- **A strong intraday up bar fades the next day.** Paper's sign (bullish) is backwards here, as Q01's and
  A10's are: the lab keeps finding next-day reversal of strong up days on NSE F&O stocks. 29% of F01's bullish
  top five are in A02's day-bearish top five and 13% in E2A's (the day-bearish draft); 0% overlap with Q06 on
  either side, so the body and the wicks really are different readings.
- **No usable flip.** Mirror arithmetic: gross move of the bullish picks -0.64% + 0.415% = -0.23%; called bearish,
  about +0.23% - 0.25% futures cost = -0.02% after costs, so a flip would have an edge near +2.7 but fail the
  bearish table's cost test. A full down body (bearish side) does not bounce enough in the top five to matter.
- **Ties do not decide the top five.** Exact +1 scores appear on 0.14 symbols per date and -1 on 0.41; the 5th and
  6th scores tie on 7 of 1,482 bullish dates and 13 of 1,484 bearish dates.
