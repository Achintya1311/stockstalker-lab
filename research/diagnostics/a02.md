# A02 (extreme-day reversal): train diagnosis

`cards/A02.yml`: after a day whose close-to-close return is >=2 sigma (20-day trailing, excluding the
extreme day itself) from its symbol's trailing volatility, score the opposite sign for the next session
(day horizon only). Catalog's own claim is not tied to one paper ("microstructure literature", no single
dated source), so there is no vintage out-of-sample check to run — only the train numbers themselves.
Source: `experiments/done/a02-extreme-day-reversal-day/results.{json,md}`, `reports/evidence_train.md`'s
day/bearish and day/bullish tables (block-bootstrap + SPA/StepM/MCS).

## The headline (top / 5-pick selection, train 2015-2020)

| Side | Edge (pts) | t | 95% bootstrap interval | Positive years | Coverage | Dir. move after costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bullish | -1.10 | -1.07 | -2.97 to +0.87 | 3 of 6 (50%) | 74.1% | -0.63% |
| Bearish | **+4.34** | **5.24** | **+2.62 to +6.06** | **6 of 6 (100%)** | 88.5% | -0.04% |

No leak alarm on either side (58.5% long-sample accuracy, well under the 70%/15pt trigger in SPEC.md
section 6).

## Reading it

**Bearish works, bullish doesn't — this is not a symmetric reversal effect.** After a big up day, fading
to bearish beats the market line by 4.34 points with t=5.24 and a 95% interval that clears zero by a wide
margin (StepM names it individually superior; it's in `reports/evidence_train.md`'s day/bearish table,
3rd of 27 experiments by edge). Every one of the 6 train years is individually positive, from +1.79
(2019) to +8.21 (2016) — this is the most consistent year-by-year record of any card diagnosed so far in
this lab, not just a strong average pulled up by one good year.

After a big down day, fading to bullish is a loser: -1.10 points, t=-1.07 (not distinguishable from zero),
and only 3 of 6 years positive — 2017 and 2020 are clearly negative (-4.70, -8.01), 2015 is the best year
(+4.46) but nowhere near enough to carry the average. The catalog's claim ("after a very large down day,
bullish next day") does not survive contact with the data; the catalog's matching bearish half does.

**Coverage is asymmetric too, consistent with volatility clustering being asymmetric**: bearish fires on
88.5% of dates vs bullish's 74.1%. Big up days (which this strategy fades to bearish) are simply more
common or more extreme relative to trailing sigma than big down days are in this universe over this
period — the kind of skew the raw rule (z >= 2 either direction) doesn't correct for.

**Directional move after costs is the catch, even on the winning side.** Bearish's hit-rate edge
(+4.34pts, highly significant) comes with a directional move of only -0.04% after costs — essentially
flat, not meaningfully money-making even though the direction is right more often than the market line.
This is the same "hit rate works, P&L doesn't" shape `state/queue.md`'s confluence lane was built to
investigate for exactly this family (A02 is one of its two base strategies, alongside D01). The confluence
lane's own finding stands: **E2A** (A02 restricted to OI-rising/crowded moves) turns this same day-bearish
edge into a positive directional move (+0.127%) while improving the edge further (+7.46pts) — i.e. open
interest, not the reversal signal itself, is what makes A02's bearish side worth anything beyond hit-rate
bragging rights. See `research/diagnostics/confluence.md` for the full E2A/E2B comparison.

**Sector / weekday breakdown: not available from this run.** `picks.json` carries only `{date, symbol,
score}`, not outcomes or sector, and this sandbox never holds the joined dataset (`SPEC.md`'s "Data"
section — the full dataset lives only in the Actions cache), the same limitation `o01.md`/`o02.md`/`o03.md`
already flagged. Worth asking a supervised or Actions-side run to add a sector/weekday cut to
`engine/experiment_report.py` directly if this card reaches a validation query.

## Go-live track relevance

A02-bearish (day) has a positive train edge (+4.34) but its own directional move after costs is ~flat
(-0.04%), not positive — so by the go-live provisional rule (SPEC.md section 10: "positive train edge
*and* positive train directional move... for that table's horizon and side"), **A02 alone does not
qualify A02-bearish as a provisional candidate for day-bearish**. Its confluence-lane descendant **E2A**
does clear both bars (edge +7.46, directional move +0.127%) and is already flagged as a P1 candidate in
`state/queue.md`, queued for a validation query once holdout queries open for this lane (Mon 26 Oct). A02
