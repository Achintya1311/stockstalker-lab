## Where it works and fails

- **Bullish is one of the lab's most reliably wrong-way readings**: negative in every year, t -8.51. 50% of
  its top five are A02's day-bearish picks and 37% are T20's day-bullish picks (also wrong-way, -3.75). A
  close far above the prior bar's typical price is a big up day, and big up days reverse next day here.
- **Bearish is also wrong-way**, in five of six years: big down closes tend to bounce, A02's bullish
  reading, but not by enough to call bullish (A02 day-bullish -1.10).
- A flip of the bullish side would re-test A02's bearish side (+4.34, dir -0.04%), which already misses the
  cost test. Not queued.
