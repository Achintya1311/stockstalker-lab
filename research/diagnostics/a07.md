# A07: high-volume return premium (Gervais, Kaniel and Mingelgrin 2001): diagnosis

Train split (2015-01-01 to 2020-12-31), week horizon, `top` selection (5 picks/table/date) unless noted.
Source replay: `experiments/done/a07-high-volume-premium-week/`. Joint test: `reports/evidence_train.md`.

## What the card tests

The paper finds US stocks with unusually high recent volume, relative to their own history, earn higher
returns over the following weeks. `cards/A07.yml` scores a plain volume z-score: last 5 sessions' mean
volume against the prior 50 sessions' mean and standard deviation. High z is bullish, low z bearish. The
score is already signed around zero, so unlike A04-A06 it needed no centring, and both sides fire.

tighter: bullish -0.52 (t -1.60, 2/6 years), bearish -0.45 (t -1.52, 1/6 years). So the published
direction is, if anything, reversed at NSE's one-week horizon: a volume spike is mildly followed by
underperformance and a volume drought mildly by outperformance. Neither reading is significant.

-0.84, P(edge<=0)=0.776), in cells whose SPA p-values (0.769, 0.190) say nothing in them is real.
