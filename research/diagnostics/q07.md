## Pre-declared rule

Predicted day bearish +1.5 to +3.5 with a directional move near zero, day bullish 0 to +2, week bearish +1 to +3,
week bullish near zero. **All four held.** Helps (bearish: +2.0, interval above 0, 4 of 6 years, positive
directional move; bullish: +2.0, interval above 0, 4 of 6 years): **not met** on any side. Day bearish misses only
the cost test (-0.02%), week bearish the interval and the cost test.

## Where it works and fails

- **A close stretched above its own trend line fades the next day, by just under the futures cost.** Gross move
  about +0.23% (-0.02% + 0.25%), against Q06R's +0.26% and F02's +0.28%.
- **It is the reversal family under another name.** Approximate top-5 overlap, day bearish (no universe filter):
  24.6% with F02, 23.5% with T06 (Bollinger %B), 19.9% with T13 (Williams %R), 17.1% with A02 and 7.4% with E2A.
  Week bearish: 44.5% with A01 (the week-bearish draft), 3.8% with Q05.
- **Pullbacks below the line do not bounce enough.** Day bullish is noise, week bullish slightly wrong-way, as the
  lab's bullish reversal readings usually are.
