## Where it works and fails

- **Distance from a trailing stop picks the most extended stocks**, the same group that lags in T09 (supertrend),
  T05, T08 and champion v1. 38% of T16's bullish top five are in T09's on both horizons (Jaccard 24%), and 20%
  of the day-bullish top five are champion v1's.
- The bullish side is wrong-way in every year on day and five of six on week, so the extended group lags
  reliably, but the mirror (calling them bearish) is what A02 and T05C already test.
- The bearish side, stocks far below a falling stop, is noise on both horizons.
