## Where it works and fails

- **Day bullish is another "edge but not cost" day-bullish reading.** Its interval excludes zero (P(edge<=0)
  0.009), but with 31 day-bullish trials StepM does not name it individually superior (it names D01C, T04 and
  T10-NR7). Gross move about -0.08% a day, so it misses the 0.415% delivery cost by about 0.5 percentage points, like the
  others.
- **New stocks:** 0% of its day-bullish top five are T04's, 1% T10-NR7's, 3% D01C's, 4% T01's.
- **Ranking adds a little:** `all` day bullish is +0.68 against +1.85 for the top five, so the strongest bodies
  carry most of it.
- Week bullish is lumpy (2019 and 2020 carry it); 25% of its top five are T09's.
- Neither bearish side has an edge.
