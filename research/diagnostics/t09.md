## Where it works and fails

- **The loss is in the ranking, not the trend state.** Supertrend is always on, so about 70 stocks a date are
  bullish. All of them together are near zero (day -0.21, t -0.96; week -0.32). The five furthest above their
  line, the ones the table would publish, are significantly wrong-way. Stretched stocks lag, as with T05's
  up-cross, T08's breakouts and champion v1's top bullish five.
- **It shares picks with champion v1** (share of its top-5 bullish picks also in champion v1's: 25.7% day,
  24.7% week) and T08-20 (35.8% week), so this is one family: "most extended in an uptrend", read bullish.
- 2015 is the only positive year on both horizons, the same 2015 exception T05C showed from the other side.
- Bearish has no edge on either horizon.
