## Where it works and fails

- **The best 100%-coverage single day-bearish reading that also clears the directional rule after D01C.** Among
  full-coverage day-bearish strategies only D01C (+4.74, dir +0.08%) and the consensus (+4.61) read higher. Every year is
  positive; the weakest (2015 +2.81, 2017 +3.02) are still above the +2.0 bar.
- **A different set of names from the OI fade.** Top-5 bearish overlap: D01C 17%, F07 29%, T06 31%, E2A 32%, F10 28%,
  A02 44%. It shares the up-bar fade with A02 but needs no 2-sigma day, so it reads on every date.
- **The edge is the extreme.** `all` +0.98 vs `top` +4.47; the bar ratio does most of the ranking at the extremes (the
  card's second red flag), so it behaves like "biggest up bar that closes at a 10-day high".
- **The bullish side (10-day low on a down bar) is not T04's oversold reading** (9.8% overlap) and has no edge after
  costs; 2020 is negative.
