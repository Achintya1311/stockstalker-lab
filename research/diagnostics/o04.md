# O04 (order-block rank-average composite) vs O01 alone and D01 alone: train diagnosis

`state/queue.md`'s order-block lane: "once O04's train replay lands, diagnose it the same way O00-O03
were: does O04 beat O01 alone, and does it beat D01 alone the way O02's bearish cell did?" O04's replay
landed this run (`experiments/done/o04-order-block-composite-{day,week}/`). Sources: those
`results.md`/`results.json`, `experiments/done/o01-retest-{day,week}/results.md`,
`experiments/done/d01-oi-buildup-day/results.md`, `research/diagnostics/d01.md`, plus a direct
day-by-day comparison of `picks.json` for O01 and O04 (both `top`/5-pick selection, train 2015-2020).

## O04 vs O01 alone (top / 5-pick selection, train 2015-2020)

| Horizon | Side | O01 edge (t) | O04 edge (t) | Delta | Picks / dates (identical for both) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day | Bullish | -0.47 (-0.80) | **-1.16 (-1.93)** | **-0.69 (worse)** | 6,673 / 1,419 |
| Day | Bearish | +0.66 (0.95) | **-0.65 (-0.93)** | **-1.31 (worse, flips sign)** | 5,641 / 1,389 |
| Week | Bullish | -2.21 (-1.86) | **-2.95 (-2.39)** | **-0.74 (worse)** | 1,418 / 299 |
| Week | Bearish | +0.18 (0.12) | **-0.44 (-0.32)** | **-0.62 (worse, flips sign)** | 1,183 / 293 |

**O04 loses to O01 alone on every one of the four cells**, and on the two cells where O01 had the only
positive (if insignificant) edge in the whole ablation — day-bearish and week-bearish — the composite
flips the sign negative. This is the first ablation step in the lane (O00→O01→O02→O03→O04) that is
unambiguously worse than O01 on every cell at once, rather than mixed by side.

The picks-and-dates counts are *exactly* identical between O01's `top` selection and O04's `top`
selection on every cell (e.g. day-bullish: 6,673 picks on 1,419 dates for both). That is expected and
confirms the card's abstention rule worked as specified — O04 "abstains when O01 abstains," so it draws
its candidate pool from exactly the same dates and the same set of eligible symbols as O01. **The edge
difference above is entirely a re-ranking effect, not a coverage or pool-composition effect**: both
strategies choose 5 names out of the same abstaining-or-not pool each day; O04 just orders that pool by
the rank-averaged composite instead of by O01's own `|score|`, and the composite's top 5 performs worse.

## How different is the re-ranking, in practice?

Direct day-by-day comparison of the actual top-5 symbol sets (`picks.json`, both strategies, same dates):

| Horizon | Side | Common dates | Avg. overlap (Jaccard, top-5 vs top-5) | Days with identical top-5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day | Bullish | 1,426 | 44.4% | 2.1% |
| Day | Bearish | 1,413 | 73.8% | 38.9% |
| Week | Bullish | 301 | 45.2% | 1.7% |
| Week | Bearish | 298 | 73.1% | 38.3% |

The bullish side is reshuffled far more than the bearish side: fewer than half of O04's bullish top-5
on a typical day is the same five names O01 alone would have published, and an identical top-5 happens
on barely 2% of days. Bearish re-ranking is milder (about three-quarters overlap), which lines up with
D01's bearish signal being the less-wrong of D01's two sides (see below) — pulling in a somewhat-less-bad
ingredient does less damage than pulling in a very-wrong one.

## Does O04 beat D01 alone? (day horizon only — D01 has no week variant)

| Side | D01 alone edge (t) | O04 (3-way composite) edge (t) | O04 beats D01 by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bullish | -4.61 (-8.15) | -1.16 (-1.93) | **+3.45 (much better)** |
| Bearish | -2.48 (-4.59) | -0.65 (-0.93) | **+1.83 (better)** |

**Yes, clearly, on both day cells.** `o02.md`'s own table shows O02 (OB+D01 hard gate) improved on D01
alone only on bearish (+2.49) and was itself *worse* than D01 on bullish (-0.54 — O02's bullish edge of
-5.15 is more negative than D01's -4.61). O04 goes further than O02 did: it improves on D01 alone on
**both** day cells (+3.45 bullish, +1.83 bearish), the first order-block composite in the lane to do so
on bullish as well as bearish. But this is a low bar: D01 alone is a confidently wrong signal on both
sides (0% positive years per `d01.md`), so almost anything that dilutes it with less-wrong ingredients —
O01's near-zero score, a volume z-score — will look better by comparison. The real question the lane
asks is whether O04 beats *O01*, not whether it beats the worse of its three ingredients — and on that
question the answer above is no.

## Where it fails, by year

Per `results.json`'s `by_year` (`top` selection):

- **Day-bullish:** O04 is negative in 5 of 6 years (2016-2020), positive only in 2015 (+1.61, smaller
  than O01's own 2015 of +2.16). 2020 is the worst year for both O01 (-2.42) and O04 (-2.74).
- **Day-bearish:** O04 is negative in 4 of 6 years (2016, 2017, 2019, 2020); 2015 and 2018 are the only
  positive years (+0.80, +1.57) — both smaller than O01's own 2015 (+1.35) and 2018 (+3.86). O01's best
  year, 2018, is where O04 loses the most ground to O01 (3.86 → 1.57).
- **Week-bullish:** O04 is negative in 5 of 6 years; 2019 is its worst year by far (-7.77), far below
  O01's own 2019 (-1.23) or D01's absence of a week variant to compare against.
- **Week-bearish:** O04 is positive only in 2018 (+4.71, close to O01's own best year there, +5.46) and
  negative in the other five; O01's second-best year, 2016 (+2.13), becomes negative for O04 (-1.20).

**Pattern:** O04 does not fail uniformly across years — it tends to erode O01's own best years (2018
bearish, 2015 bullish) more than it changes O01's already-bad years, consistent with the re-ranking
picture above: on the days where O01's raw `|score|` would have surfaced its most profitable names, the
composite's extra OI/volume ingredients push different, weaker names to the top instead.

## What this means for "do order blocks help?"

**No, and O04 is the clearest negative result in the ablation so far.** Blending O01's own proximity
score with D01's OI build-up and a volume z-score, then re-ranking by that blend instead of by O01's own
confidence, makes every one of the four cells worse than just reading O01 alone — including flipping
both previously-positive cells (day-bearish, week-bearish) negative. The composite does improve on D01
in isolation, but that is because D01 alone is badly wrong, not because the composite adds anything: a
weighted average of one near-zero signal (O01) and one confidently-wrong signal (D01), rank-averaged and
then taken at face value, predictably regresses toward the wrong signal rather than toward the better
one. This is a different failure mode from O02/O03's "mostly subsets O01's own picks" (O03's finding):
O04 draws from exactly O01's pool (by construction) but *actively reorders it for the worse*, which is a
stronger and cleaner negative result than a near-subset that merely fails to add anything.
