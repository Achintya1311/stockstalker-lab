## Where it works and fails

- **A rise given back on a wide bar keeps falling the next day.** The gross move of the bearish picks is about
  +0.26% (+0.01% + 0.25% futures cost), against Q06's +0.20%: wider bars carry bigger follow-through, just enough
  to clear the cost. The margin is one basis point, so a small cost change would erase it.
- **A dip bought back on a wide bar is weaker than on any bar.** Bullish fell from +2.58 to +1.88 and 2020 is
  flat. The likely reading: a wide bar with a long lower wick is often a high-volatility selloff day, which the
  lab's reversal readings already find is noisy next day.
- **Overlap.** Approximate top-5 overlap (no universe filter): bearish 41.8% with Q06 bearish, 2.6% with A09,
  1.1% with E2A (the day-bearish draft). Bullish 37.8% with Q06, 1.4% with T04.
