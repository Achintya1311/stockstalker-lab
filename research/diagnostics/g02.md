## Where it works and fails

- **Two years carry it.** 2018 and 2019 give +9.61 and +5.21 bullish, +6.37 and +8.15 bearish; 2016, 2017 and 2020
  are near zero or negative on both sides. 2018-19 were the years broad mid- and small-cap stocks fell while a few
  large defensives held up, which is exactly when low beta wins on raw hit rate. 2020's crash and rebound reversed it.
- **Few distinct stocks.** Beta moves slowly, as the card's red flag said: the 1,300 bullish picks use 32 distinct
  stocks, the bearish 39. The edge is a bet on a handful of names, so the t of about 2 overstates the evidence.
- **Not the low-volatility signal.** Top-5 bullish overlap: 4.1% with A06, 23.2% with I02, 3.5% with A05B (the draft),
  1.5% with G01, 1.3% with G03. Bearish: 6.2% with A01, 6.3% with G01. Beta picks different stocks from the
  volatility readings, so it is a separate family, but its edge is regime-bound.
