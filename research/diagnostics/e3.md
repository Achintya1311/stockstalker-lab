# E3A / E3B: A02 split by H02's stock-specific volatility, diagnosis

Train split (2015-01-01 to 2020-12-31), day horizon, `top` selection. Replays: `experiments/done/e3a-a02-high-ivol-day/`,
`experiments/done/e3b-a02-low-ivol-day/`. Cards: `cards/E3A.yml`, `cards/E3B.yml`. Rule: A02's extreme-day reversal
(|z| >= 2 against the 20-day sigma) kept only where H02's 20-session one-factor residual volatility is above (E3A) or
below (E3B) that date's median. Both arms were added to `ledger/trials.csv` together, before any result was read.

## Pre-declared rule (card, 5 Nov)

- E3A day bearish +3.5 to +5.5: **held** (+3.97). On 75 to 95% of A02's bearish pick-dates: **held** (1,145 of 1,273, 90%).
- E3A day bullish -2.5 to 0: **held** (-1.35).
- E3B day bearish -1.0 to +5.0: **missed high** (+5.30). On 10 to 40% of A02's bearish pick-dates: **missed high**
  (1,050 of 1,273, 82%). The guess that an extreme day pushes most stocks into the high-ivol half was wrong: the `all`
  selection splits A02's bearish signals 4,275 / 4,141, about half each.
- Helps (E3A at least +5.34, interval above 0, positive directional move, above E3B): **not met** on three counts
  (+3.97, dir -0.06%, below E3B). Stop rule: a **null**; the high-ivol confluence A02 x H02 is closed.

## Where it works and fails

- **The hypothesis came out backwards.** Extreme up-days on calm stocks fade more reliably than on volatile ones. E3B is
  positive every year within +3.39 to +7.61; E3A's average is carried by 2016-17 (+10.16, +6.28) and sits under +2.0 in
  the other four years. A plausible reading: on a calm stock a 2-sigma day is more likely a liquidity shock than news,
  while on a volatile stock it is more often information that sticks. This is a post-hoc story from the control arm, not
  a tested claim.
- **Top-5 bearish overlap** (from the pick logs, before the engine's eligibility filter): E3A with A02 71%, E3B with A02
  53%, E3A with E3B 0% by construction; with E2A, E3A 48% and E3B 41%. Most of E3B's gain over A02 comes from dropping
  the volatile half, not from new names.
- **No directional case.** Both arms have a negative directional move after the 0.25% futures cost; only E2A (+0.13%)
  clears it in this family.
- **No table change.** E3B (+5.30) is below E2A (+7.46 train) and far below the +7.72 bar that a query to replace E2A
  needs. Bullish sides are noise.

## What to try next

- E3B is a post-hoc winner from a two-arm split: anything built on it must be a new pre-declared card. The natural one is
  E2A restricted to below-median ivol (crowded move on a calm stock); pre-declare that it must beat E2A's +7.46 by at
  least +1 with positive directional move, else close. Its overlap with E2A (41% for E3B) suggests it would cut E2A's
  coverage to roughly half, so a positive reading also needs a coverage check (at least 50% of E2A's dates).
- Do not rank-average E3B with E2A (past consensus readings landed below their strongest member).
