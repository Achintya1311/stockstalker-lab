# A08 (overnight/intraday tug-of-war, Lou-Polk-Skouras 2019): train diagnosis

`cards/A08.yml`: `score = avg_intraday - avg_overnight` over a trailing 20-day window of each day's
close-to-open (`overnight`) and open-to-close (`intraday`) legs, on split/bonus-adjusted prices. Bullish
when the recent return has come mostly from the intraday leg; bearish when it has come mostly from the
overnight leg. Source: `experiments/done/a08-overnight-intraday-tug-day/results.{json,md}`,
`reports/evidence_train.md`'s day/bearish and day/bullish tables (block-bootstrap + SPA/StepM/MCS).

## The headline (top / 5-pick selection, train 2015-2020)

| Side | Edge (pts) | t | 95% bootstrap interval | Positive years | Coverage | Dir. move after costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bullish | +0.04 | 0.07 | -1.02 to +1.15 | 1 of 6 (17%) | 100.0% | -0.51% |

Edge-t uses the raw per-trial t, not SPEC.md section 6 gate 2's trial-corrected bar. No leak alarm on
either side (both well under 70% accuracy / 15pt edge).

## Reading it

**Bearish: small, oddly consistent, not significant.** +1.15 points over the market line, t=1.97 —
short of even the uncorrected t≥3 bar, let alone gate 2's trial-adjusted one. The bootstrap interval's
lower bound (-0.00) rounds to touching zero; `reports/evidence_train.md` puts P(edge<=0) at 0.025, low
but not enough to earn a StepM "individually superior" tag the way a02/a09/d01c/e1b/e2a/e2b/t01/t04/t06/
t13/t15 do on the same table. What makes this cell worth a second look despite the weak significance is
the year-by-year shape: **all 6 train years are individually positive** (2015 +1.75, 2016 +2.56, 2017
+1.49, 2018 +0.54, 2019 +0.52, 2020 +0.13) — monotonically shrinking, but never once crossing zero. That
is a genuinely unusual pattern next to this family's other members (A02, A09 both have real negative
years mixed in). A small, shrinking-over-time edge that is directionally this stable is more likely a
real but weak and decaying effect than noise that happened to land on the positive side six times by
chance — though six years is a thin sample for that claim on its own.

**Bullish: not distinguishable from zero, and the one good year does the work.** +0.04 points, t=0.07,
P(edge<=0)=0.455 — a coin flip. 2020 alone (+3.83) is the only positive year; every other year (2015
-0.98, 2016 -0.36, 2017 -0.64, 2018 -0.05, 2019 -1.73) is flat-to-negative. This reads the same way A02's
and A09's bullish sides did: one strong year propping up an otherwise negative or flat average, not a
real effect.

**Directional move after costs: negative on both sides, same as the rest of this family.** Bearish's
-0.04% sits in exactly the "hit rate works, money doesn't" bucket this lane has seen in A02 (-0.04%) and
confirmed in A09 (the one exception, at +0.03%) — A08 does not repeat A09's exception; it lands back on
the losing side, by a hair. Bullish's -0.51% is unambiguously money-losing even setting hit rate aside.

**The paper's own mechanism did not clearly transfer, and the family-correlation red flag did not hold
either.** `cards/A08.yml`'s own red flags expected A08 and A09 to end up "highly correlated" since both
trade the overnight-vs-intraday split, just from different literatures. Computed directly from both
cards' `picks.json` pick logs (day-by-day top-5 Jaccard overlap, bearish side, 1,449 shared dates): **mean
overlap is 2.1%**, with zero dates where the two cards' top-5 bearish lists are identical. Bullish overlap
is similarly low (2.5%). For comparison, A08-bearish vs A02-bearish overlaps at 0.6% — lower still. So
A08, A09 and A02 are reading genuinely different stocks each day despite all three nominally touching the
"overnight vs intraday" or "big-move fade" idea — the catalog's grouping of these as one "family" by
mechanism name does not mean they pick the same names, which is useful to know ahead of SPEC.md section
6 gate 8's pairwise 50%-overlap check: none of these three would collide with each other there even if
all three somehow cleared the other gates.

**Sector / weekday breakdown: not available from this run** — same limitation already flagged in
`a02.md`/`a09.md`/`o01.md`-`o05.md`: `picks.json` carries only `{date, symbol, score}`, and this sandbox
never holds the joined dataset.
