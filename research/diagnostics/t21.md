## Pre-declared rule and prediction

- Rule: **not met** on either side.
- Prediction (week-bullish top five negative, as T08's 55-day breakout): **wrong.** +1.66, positive every year
  from 2017. Only 20% of T21's bullish top five are T08-55's; the template's SMA and 30%-off-the-low conditions
  select a different set from a plain breakout.

## The top five are mostly a tie-break

The card flagged this before the replay. **71% of the bullish top-five picks score exactly +1** (a close at the
52-week high), and on **43% of dates more than five stocks are tied at +1**, so the five shown are chosen by the
order among tied stocks, not by the signal. The bullish number is therefore closer to "a random five of the
template's stocks at a new high" than to a ranked top five. A sharper conviction score (for example the RS
rating, the template's own eighth point) would be needed before reading the bullish edge as the template's.

## Where it works and fails

- The bullish gross move is about -0.31% a week: positive hit rate, negative mean move, far from the 0.415%
  delivery cost.
- The bearish mirror is flat; 2019 (-4.93) is the one bad year.
- Overlap of the bullish top five: 24% with I01's, 16% with T02's.
