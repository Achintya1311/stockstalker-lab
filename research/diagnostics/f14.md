## Where it works and fails

- **The bullish side is the useful one.** A 3-session fall inside a rising 100-session trend is bullish next day in all
  six years, and the `all` selection carries nearly the same edge (+2.00), so it is not a top-five artefact. StepM names
  it individually superior on day bullish (8 of 63). Like every day-bullish reading, the move after the 0.415% delivery
  cost is negative (-0.52%).
- **A different oversold reading from T04.** Bullish top-5 overlap: T04 19%, F10 10%, D01C 10%, T10-NR7 2%, Q06 2%. T04
  needs RSI(2) below 10 above the 200-day mean; F14 takes the largest 3-session falls in any rising 100-session trend
  and has picks on 87% of scored dates against T04's 74% (evidence report).
- **The bearish side is a slow-reversion reading.** Names far above their 100-session low with a flat mean lose next day
  a little more often than the market (+2.34), but the moves are small (dir +0.014%). It shares almost no names with the
  fade family: F11 8%, F13 7%, A02 4%, E2A 4%. 2016 (-0.20) is the only negative year.
