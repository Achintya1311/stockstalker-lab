# F08: seven-session reversal weighted by the yearly move (Alpha#19), diagnosis

Train split (2015-01-01 to 2020-12-31), week horizon, `top` selection. Replay: `experiments/done/f08-weighted-reversal-week/`.
Card: `cards/F08.yml`. Rule: minus the sign of the 7-session change, times (1 + the date's percentile rank of the
250-session summed return). A 7-session fall is bullish, a rise bearish; last year's best performers get the largest
weight, so each side's top five are the strongest yearly performers carrying that side's 7-session sign.

## Pre-declared rule (card, 5 Nov)

- Week bearish -1.0 to +3.0: **held** (+0.58); dir -0.15% to +0.15%: **held** (+0.08%).
- Week bullish -2.0 to +2.0 with a negative dir: edge **missed high by 0.04** (+2.04); dir **held** (-0.23%).
- Top-5 bearish overlap with A01 10 to 35%: **held, at the floor** (10.1%). Top-5 bullish overlap with A03's bullish
  picks 25 to 60%: **held** (44.1%; 39.1% with CAIRN dropped, below).
- Helps, bearish (+2.0, interval above 0, 4 of 5 years, positive dir): **not met**. A01 stays on week bearish.
- Helps, bullish (AB's edge-only rule: +2.0, interval above 0, 4 of 5 years): edge met by 0.04; interval (-0.81)
  and years (3/5) **not met**. No query; A05B stays on week bullish.

## Where it works and fails

- **Bullish is a 2019 reading.** +7.90 in 2019 carries the average; without it the four other years average +0.56.
  2019 was the year a few large caps (last year's winners) kept rising while the broad F&O list fell, so buying the
  strongest yearly names on a 7-session dip was a large-cap-leadership bet that year, not a reversal.
- **The reversal does not survive the weighting on the bearish side.** A01 fades last week's biggest rise and earns
  +2.48 train; F08's bearish five are last year's winners that rose over 7 sessions, only 10% the same names as A01,
  and the edge falls to +0.58 with 2019 at -3.39 (the same large-cap year, now on the wrong side).
- **Close to momentum on the bullish side.** 44% of the bullish top five are A03's 12-1 momentum picks and 30% H05's;
  0.7% are H02's and 1.2% A05B's. On the week-bullish table it sits sixth of 55 by train edge, behind G02, T25, G01,
  A05B and H02; the joint test finds no week-bullish strategy individually superior (SPA p 0.247, StepM none).
- **No joint support.** Neither side is StepM superior; week bearish's only StepM name is H02.
- **Another stale symbol.** CAIRN (Cairn India, merged into Vedanta in 2017) is in the raw bullish top five on 150 of
  260 dates and scores on every week through 2020-12; the earlier five stale names (SKSMICRO 114 dates, ABIRLANUVO 65)
  fill 13.8% of the raw bearish top five. The engine's universe filter keeps them out of the scored picks (5 per date);
  overlaps above drop all six. Added to the WATCH entry in `state/engine-issues.md`.
