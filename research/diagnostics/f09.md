## Where it works and fails

- **Volume does not confirm the oversold reading.** The bullish top five are a different set from T04 (8.3%) and F10
  (7.2%), and read at about a quarter of T04's +3.37. A heavy-turnover fall on this universe is as often news that keeps
  going as a washout that reverses.
- **The bearish side (light turnover after a rise) is noise**, wrong-way in four of six years, and shares 0.7% of names
  with F11's range-top fade: quiet rises are not the fading kind; loud ones (F11, A02) are.
- **Coarse score.** Both ranks take 20 and 8 values, so a median of two stocks tie at the top score each date; the
  tie-break decides one pick in five. Not a reason for the null (the `all` selection is flat as well).
