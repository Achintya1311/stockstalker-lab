## Where it works and fails

- **"Breakouts on volume continue" is the opposite of what happens here.** A stock closing near its high on twice
  its usual volume underperforms the next day. This is A02's extreme-day reversal seen from the other side:
  **52% of T20's bullish top five are in A02's day-bearish top five** (19% in E2A's). A02 already fades these
  stocks, with the edge on the bearish side.
- **A flip would not pay.** The bullish gross move is about -0.20% a day, so read as bearish it would gross about
  +0.20% against the 0.25% futures cost, and it would mostly duplicate A02. Not queued.
- The bearish side (heavy-volume close at the low) is near the cost line (-0.06% after costs) but noisy, with two
  losing years.
