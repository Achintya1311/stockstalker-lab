# T05: MACD(12, 26, 9) crossover: diagnosis

Train split (2015-01-01 to 2020-12-31), `top` selection unless noted. Replays:
`experiments/done/t05-macd-crossover-week/` and `.../t05-macd-crossover-day/`. Card: `cards/T05.yml`. Joint
test: `reports/evidence_train.md`.

## What the card tests

The catalog's rule is an event: bullish on the bar where the MACD line crosses above its signal line,
bearish on a cross below, abstain otherwise. Score = (MACD - signal) / close on the crossing bar, so the
top five are the sharpest fresh crosses. A median date has about 7 fresh up-crosses and 5 down-crosses
among the scored stocks, so the week table is usually close to full (coverage 91%).

## Where it works and fails

- **Fresh up-crosses are reliably followed by underperformance.** This is the lab's clearest wrong-way
  result: the bullish side's interval is entirely below zero on both horizons (week P(edge<=0) 0.991, day
  1.000), and every year from 2016 is negative. The textbook bullish signal is, on NSE F&O stocks at one
  day to one week, a lagging one.
- **Fresh down-crosses lag too, but more weakly.** Week bearish is positive (+2.02) with the largest
  after-cost directional move of any week-bearish reading in the lab (+0.23%, above D02-contrarian's
  +0.16%), but its interval straddles zero and the first two years were negative.
- So the honest reading is "a stock that has just had a MACD cross in either direction tends to lag the
  following week", not "MACD calls direction". Up-cross stocks have just rallied, which fits short-term
  reversal (A01). But T05's picks are not A01's: 0% overlap with A01-bearish's top five, 0.5% with T15,
  2.1% with D02-contrarian, 0.2% with the week-bearish consensus.

## Go-live

T05 week-bearish meets the provisional rule's letter (1,008 picks, +2.02, +0.23%) and was missing from the
P1 list, so it is added to the week-bearish validation batch. It is the most independent of the
week-bearish readings (near-zero overlap with every other one), which makes it a natural second-version
member for a week-bearish consensus. Caveat: P(edge<=0) is 0.113 and the joint test clears nothing in the
week/bearish cell (SPA p=0.198).

No bullish side qualifies (both negative).
