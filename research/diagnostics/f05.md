# F05: change in close location (Alpha#53): diagnosis

Train split (2015-01-01 to 2020-12-31), day horizon, `top` selection. Replay:
`experiments/done/f05-close-location-change-day/`. Card: `cards/F05.yml`. Rule: loc = ((close - low) - (high -
close)) / (close - low); score = minus its change over 9 sessions.

## Pre-declared rule

Predicted both sides within ±1.5 of zero, directional moves negative. **Held.** Helps: **not met.**

## Where it works and fails

- **The top five are a near-low detector, as the card warned.** Approximate top-5 overlap (no universe filter):
  30.5% of the bullish picks are F01's bearish picks (bars that closed near their low), 7.0% Q06's bearish. Those
  bars are noise the next day in F01 too (+0.17).
- **The ranking, not the idea, fails.** `all` beats `top` on both sides, the opposite of a useful score. A bounded
  form (location over the bar's range) would rank by the change itself; it would be a new trial and is the
  obvious one if this family is revisited.
