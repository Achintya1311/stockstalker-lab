## Where it works and fails

- **Both crossings are wrong-way at the day horizon.** The module tests the card's pairing literally
  (`cards/T14.yml` flags it as unusual), and both events point the wrong way: a stock climbing back above -100
  keeps lagging, and a stock dropping back below +100 keeps rising more often than the market.
- **Same shape as T12.** T12's stochastic up-cross from oversold is -4.53 (0/6 years, `t12.md`); T14's up-cross
  is -2.44 (0/6). Only 19% of T14's day-bullish top five are in T12's, so this is the same behaviour seen
  through a different oscillator, not the same stocks.
- **Waiting for the cross loses what the level had.** T13's Williams %R fades the overbought level itself and
  is +2.89 day-bearish (6/6, `t13.md`). T14 waits for CCI to come back below +100 and is -1.29. In this
  universe the edge, such as it is, is in fading the stretch while it lasts, not the turn after it.
- Not a ranking effect: `all` matches `top` on both day sides.
