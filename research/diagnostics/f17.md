## Where it works and fails

- **Heavy selling into the close continues the next day.** The bullish five (closes at the low on the day's heaviest
  turnover, just after a 10-session high) keep falling: wrong-way in five of six years, worst in 2020 (-6.09). The fresh
  high does not mark them as a pullback inside a rise.
- **Flipped, it would be thin.** Mirror arithmetic for an F17 bullish-side flip scored bearish: gross move about -0.29%
  per pick (-0.70% plus the 0.415% delivery cost), so about +0.04% after the 0.25% futures cost, at about +2.4 edge. That
  is well below the day-bearish fade family (F13 +4.72, E2A +7.46) and would not change any table, so no flip card is
  queued; noted for a future day-bearish consensus if one needs an independent member (overlap under 6% with all of them).
