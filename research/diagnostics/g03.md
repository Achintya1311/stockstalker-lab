## Where it works and fails

- **The smoothness filter helps hit rate a little, not the move.** Against plain 12-1 momentum (A03: bullish +0.21,
  bearish +0.85) G03 adds about 1.3 points bullish and 2 points bearish, with 55% and 42% top-5 overlap. But its
  bearish directional move is -0.44%: its misses must be larger than its hits, so
  the hit rate improves while the average move goes the wrong way.
- **Bullish is one year.** 2019 (+7.52) is the only clearly positive year; without it the bullish edge is below zero.
- **Close to G01 on the bearish side** (28.5% overlap) and weaker in every way: G01 is +4.22 with a directional move
  of -0.05%. Residual volatility scaling picks steadier losers than counting down days does.
