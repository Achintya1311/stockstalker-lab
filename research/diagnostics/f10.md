Card: `cards/F10.yml`. Rule: centred sum of two cross-sectional ranks: calm last two sessions relative to the last five
(one minus the rank of the 2- over 5-session return deviation) and a one-day reversal (one minus the rank of today's
change). High = bullish, low = bearish.

## Where it works and fails

- **Bearish is steady but not new.** Every year positive, weakest 2019-20 (+1.34, +2.19), the years the reversal family
  also softened. Overlap with the other one-day reversals sits at 22 to 30% (E2A 22%, F02 28%, F11 28%, A02 31%,
  F03 32%): the volatility-ratio half moves the names around but the edge level is the family's usual +3 to +4.
- **Bullish is independent of T04.** Only 4.9% of F10's bullish top five are T04's picks, and F10 reads on every date,
  including the 26% where T04 abstains. It is the strongest 100%-coverage bullish reading after Q06 (+2.58) and D01C (+2.54)
  that is not a fallback, but 2015 and 2020 are negative and the 0.415% delivery cost leaves dir at -0.54%.
- **Not concentrated:** both pieces are ranks of returns; the top fives spread over about 235 symbols, the most frequent
  name at 3.9% (bullish) and 1.7% (bearish) of picks.
