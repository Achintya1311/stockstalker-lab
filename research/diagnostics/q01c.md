## Where it works and fails

- **No weak year.** Every year sits between +2.4 and +4.1, the mirror of Q01 week-bullish's -2.5 to -4.4.
- **A new set of stocks.** Approximate top-5 overlap (no universe filter): 0.4% with A01 (the week-bearish draft),
  0.5% with A01_I01F, 1.0% with D02-contrarian, 1.4% with T05C, 2.3% with I01, 3.9% with T15, 5.5% with Q05. A01
  fades last week's winners; Q01C bets that last month's quiet losers keep losing. They almost never pick the same stock.
- **Joint test.** Fourth in the week-bearish cell by edge (behind A01_I01F +4.17, T05C +4.02, D02-contrarian +3.75),
  but with the narrowest interval of the four that sits above 0 and the only full coverage among them. The cell's
  SPA p is 0.127 and StepM names none individually superior at 44 trials, so this is a strong reading in a weak
  cell, not a cleared hurdle.
- **The flip was chosen after seeing Q01**, so it is a researcher degree of freedom. It is logged as its own
  trial; only validation can confirm it.
