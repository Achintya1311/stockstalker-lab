Expected about +3.2. Helps if edge at least +3.0, interval above 0, at least 4 of 6 positive years and bullish
picks on at least 99% of train dates. **Edge, interval and years met; coverage not met** (96.8%, 1,420 of

## Where it works and fails

- **The blank dates are the universe-check margin the card flagged.** On 47 dates T04 had a bullish pick that
  looked in the universe on the last visible row but was not in the engine's pick-date universe, so the switch
  chose T04 and the table came out empty. That is down from about 26% blank (T04 alone) to 3.2%, but not to 1%.
- **Q06 does better on T04's silent days than on average.** Approximate arithmetic (the per-date series is not in
  this sandbox): if the 1,087 T04 dates keep T04's +3.37, the other 333 dates average about +5.4, against Q06's
  +2.58 over all dates. The likely reading: T04 is silent when few uptrend stocks are oversold, and a bought-back
  dip is more telling on such days. This is a guess from aggregate numbers, not a measured split.
- 72% of its top-5 picks are T04's and 30% are Q06's (approximate overlap, no universe filter), as built.
- The directional move after costs stays negative (-0.42%), as both members' do: right more often than the
  market line, by moves too small to pay the 0.415% delivery cost.

## Go-live

Eligible for day bullish under AB's edge-only rule (5,621 train picks, positive edge). One validation query
(bullish only, 1 row) is queued. Decision rule fixed before the answer: it replaces T04 (released +4.36) in the
draft only if its released edge is higher. If it comes in lower, it is still the full-coverage option, and the
choice between a higher edge and a table that is blank about a quarter of days goes to AB.
