## Where it works and fails

- **A quiet drift up fades over the next week.** Three up closes on light recent turnover (relative to the 20-session
  mean) are bearish in every train year; the directional move after the futures cost, +0.232%, is the second highest of
  any week-bearish reading (T05C +0.236%), ahead of A01 (+0.02%) and H02 (+0.13%).
- **Fading strength: 2015-17 about +5.5, 2018-20 about +1.9.** The later years are still positive but each one alone is
  noise. Train ends before the paper's 2016 publication can be separated from this, so decay cannot be read here.
- **Independent of every other week-bearish reading.** Top-5 bearish overlap: T05C 5.6%, F06 5.0%, A01 4.4%, H02 3.5%,
  A01_I01F 3.5%, G01 2.2%, Q01C 1.6%. H02, T05C and Q01C overlap each other by 1.7-3.2% too.
- **Bullish (three down closes on heavy turnover) does not bounce**; negative in five of six years.
