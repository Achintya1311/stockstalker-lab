# A07C: high-volume premium faded (A07 with the sign flipped): diagnosis

Train split (2015-01-01 to 2020-12-31), week horizon, `top` selection. Replay:
`experiments/done/a07c-volume-fade-week/`. Card: `cards/A07C.yml`. Base card: `research/diagnostics/a07.md`.
Joint test: `reports/evidence_train.md`.

## What the card tests

A07 (volume z: last 5 sessions' mean volume against the prior 50) was wrong-way on both sides in train.
A07C is exactly `-A07`, pre-declared before its replay: a volume spike last week is bearish, a volume
drought bullish. The same stocks are picked; only the call is reversed.

## Pre-declared success rule: not met on either side

The card needed, on either side, a positive top-5 week edge **with the date-block bootstrap interval above
0** and a positive directional move after costs. Neither side's interval clears zero:

| Side | Stocks | Picks | Edge | t | 95% interval | P(edge<=0) | Positive years | Dir. move after costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bullish | volume drought | 1,510 | +0.90 | 0.79 | -1.35 to +3.08 | 0.206 | 3/6 | -0.52% |
| Bearish | volume spike | 1,510 | +1.34 | 1.00 | -1.32 to +3.80 | 0.151 | 4/6 | +0.03% |

Per-year edge, bullish: -1.32, +1.95, +0.97, +5.46, -0.18, -2.02. Bearish: +2.95, -1.40, +5.05, +2.98,
-4.93, +3.72 (2015-2020). The `all` selection is +0.46 (t 1.56) and +0.49 (t 1.52), the mirror of A07's
-0.52 / -0.45.

So the card's own conclusion applies: **A07's loss was noise, not an exploitable sign error, and the
volume-z family is closed.** The flip behaves as a mirror should (A07 bullish -1.42 on the spike stocks
becomes A07C bearish +1.34; A07 bearish -0.84 on the drought stocks becomes A07C bullish +0.90). That
confirms the scoring is consistent but adds no evidence: both versions sit inside their noise bands.

## Where it works and fails

- Bearish (spike, fade it) is right in 4 of 6 years but swings from +5.05 (2017) to -4.93 (2019), with no
  trend. Its directional move after costs is +0.03%, essentially zero.
- Bullish (drought, back it) depends on 2018 (+5.46) and loses after costs (-0.52%). Of all bullish
  readings in the lab, day or week, only C02 (40 picks, a calendar artefact per `calendar.md`) has a
  positive directional move after costs on the `top` selection, so no bullish table has a P1 candidate.
- Joint test: in the week/bearish cell (SPA p=0.198, StepM empty) A07C is 9th of 26; in week/bullish
  (SPA p=0.528) 6th of 23.

## Go-live

A07C-bearish meets the provisional rule's letter on week-bearish (1,510 picks, +1.34, +0.03%), so P1 makes
it eligible for one validation query. It is the weakest of the six eligible week-bearish readings, and its
own pre-declared test reads null, so it goes last in the batch. Bullish does not qualify (negative
directional move).

## Knock-on: O04

O04 ranks A07's volume z as one of three ingredients. Since neither sign of the volume z carries a
reliable week edge, the cleaner O04 ablation is to **drop the volume ingredient** (O04 without A07),
not to flip it. That is the order-block lane's queued follow-up, alongside "O04 without D01".
