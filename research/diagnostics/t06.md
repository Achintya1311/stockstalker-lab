## Where it works and fails

- **The upper-band fade is real on train and steady:** positive every year, interval well clear of zero, and
  StepM names it individually superior in day/bearish (SPA p=0.000). Its top five (stocks furthest above the
  upper band) do better than the average firing stock (+3.35 against +2.83).
- **It misses the provisional letter by 0.03%.** The picks' gross move is about +0.22% against the 0.25%
  futures cost. The same thin miss stops T04 bearish (-0.012%) and T13 bearish (-0.021%): three oscillator
  "overbought, fade it" readings, each with a significant edge, all landing just under the cost.
- **Bullish (lower-band rebound) is weak:** +1.04 with an interval across zero, and it does not pay the
  delivery cost (-0.63%).
- **It overlaps with D01C more than with the other oscillators:** share of its bearish top-5 picks also in
  D01C's 35.1%, T13's 27.1%, E2A's 14.5%, T04's 9.5%, A09's 7.5%, T05C's 6.8%. Stocks that close far above
  their band often also had a long build-up (price and OI up), which D01C fades.
