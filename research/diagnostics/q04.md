## Where it works and fails

- **A fresh 60-session high is not T08's breakout.** Only 29.9% of Q04's bullish top five are T08-55's bullish
  picks (approximate overlap, no universe filter): ranking by how recent the high is, against how old the low
  is, picks longer, steadier rises than a close above the prior 55-day high. They neither reverse like T08's
  breakouts nor persist.
- **Bearish is the slow-drift family again.** 20.3% overlap with Q05 week bearish (steady fallers), 2.3% with A01
  and 2.7% with Q01C. Five of six years positive and a gross move of about +0.05% (-0.20% + 0.25% futures cost):
  the same shape as Q03, Q05 and Q02 week bearish, never enough to pay the cost.
- 2019 is the weak year on bearish (-1.85), as for Q02 (-1.47) and Q01C's validation miss: the slow-drift
  readings share their bad periods, so combining them would not diversify.
