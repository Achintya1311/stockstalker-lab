# A09 (gap fade, one-day-lagged): train diagnosis

`cards/A09.yml`: the catalog's literal claim ("large gap up at the open bearish for that day's open-to-close,
large gap down bullish") needs the pick date's *own* open, which `engine/replay.py`'s truncation never
reveals (B4) — untestable as stated, and not fixable without a SPEC change to picks timing (flagged in the
card's own red flags). The version actually ported and tested here lags the mechanism by one trading day:
`gap_t = adj_open_t / adj_close_(t-1) - 1` is computed for *yesterday*, z-scored against its own trailing
20-day sigma (yesterday's gap excluded from that sigma, same exclusion `a02_extreme_day_reversal.py` uses),
and faded (`score = -z`) for *today's* open-to-close move if `|z| >= 2.0`. This tests whether yesterday's
gap-fade mechanism still has anything left over into today, not the catalog's same-session claim.
Source: `experiments/done/a09-gap-fade-day/results.{json,md}`, `reports/evidence_train.md`'s day/bearish
and day/bullish tables (block-bootstrap + SPA/StepM/MCS).

## Reading it

**Bearish survives the one-day lag; bullish doesn't — same asymmetry shape as A02, same "fade family"
member.** Yesterday's big gap-up, faded to bearish today, beats the market line by 3.21 points with
t=4.01 and a 95% interval that clears zero (+1.64 to +4.67). `reports/evidence_train.md`'s day/bearish
table lists it 7th of 27 by edge and StepM names it individually superior at the 10% level — real signal
survives the lagged reinterpretation, not just noise that happened to land on the "test" side of the
engine's causality wall.

Yesterday's big gap-down, faded to bullish today, does not clear the bar: +0.87pts, t=0.83, interval
straddles zero (-1.20 to +2.94). 4 of 6 years are nominally positive but the two negative years (2016,
2020) aren't small (-0.41, -3.68) and the average is carried by one strong year (2017, +4.11) rather than
a consistent effect — the same "doesn't survive contact with the data" verdict A02's bullish side got, for
the same underlying reason: whatever makes a big down-gap informative doesn't persist as cleanly as a big
up-gap does, in this universe and period.

**The lag matters, and this is the actual finding worth flagging**: this card's red flag already warned
that if the literal same-day effect and this one-day-lagged variant "don't share a sign or a magnitude,
confirming or refuting this card says nothing about the literal claim, only about the lagged variant." The
data can't settle that — the literal claim is structurally untestable here — but it's worth noting the
lagged bearish signal's year pattern (2015 +6.53, 2016 +4.36, 2017 +6.61, 2018 -1.31, 2019 -0.70, 2020
+4.20) tracks A02's own year pattern loosely (both strongest 2015-2017, both weak 2018-2019) rather than
looking independent — consistent with this being downstream of the same volatility-clustering regime A02
taps, approached from the gap angle instead of the close-to-close one, not a genuinely separate signal.
Worth checking real pick overlap with A02-bearish once both are candidates for the same table (SPEC.md
section 6 gate 8's 50% overlap check will catch this at promotion time regardless).

**Directional move after costs: this is the one card in the day-bearish fade family where it's not flatly
negative.** A02-bearish's directional move is -0.04% (essentially flat, "hit rate works, money doesn't" —
see `research/diagnostics/a02.md`). A09-bearish's is +0.03% — also essentially flat, but on the right side
of zero, for the first time in this family without a confluence filter. It's a razor's width above zero
(0.03 percentage points, well inside anything a single extra basis point of slippage would erase), so this
is not "A09 alone makes money" — it is "A09 alone doesn't lose money after costs while clearing a real
hit-rate edge," which is one notch better than A02 alone and worth a confluence check of its own (an
OI-crowding filter in the same shape as E2A, since that's exactly what turned A02's flat directional move
positive).

**Sector / weekday breakdown: not available from this run** — same limitation already flagged in
`a02.md`/`o01.md`/`o02.md`/`o03.md`: `picks.json` carries only `{date, symbol, score}`, and this sandbox
never holds the joined dataset.
