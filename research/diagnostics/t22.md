## Pre-declared rule

Predicted an edge between T17's -2.20 and T21's +1.66, with few picks. The edge came out higher (+5.03), but on
52 picks it is noise (t 0.70; each year has 7 to 18 picks). Helps only with at least 500 picks and a positive
directional move: **not met** on either.

## Where it works and fails

- **The checklist almost never passes**: about one stock every five weeks across the F&O universe. The volume
  lift-off and dry-up conditions on the same weekly pick date are the narrowest.
- With 52 picks the year-to-year swing (+31.53 to -8.10) is the sample, not the pattern.
