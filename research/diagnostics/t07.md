## Where it works and fails

- **It almost never fires.** The rule needs both conditions on the same bar: band width at its 126-day low
  **and** a close outside a band. A close outside the bands tends to widen them, so the two rarely meet on one bar.
  `cards/T07.yml` flagged this reading of "then" as a red flag before the replay; the result confirms it.
- With 10 and 18 picks the edges say nothing (per-year bullish values swing from -44.76 to +15.72).

## What to try next

- A version that remembers the squeeze (squeeze within the last N bars, then a break) needs cross-bar state;
  each strategy already sees its full trailing `history`, so it can be written as "width at a 126-day low on
  any of the last 5 bars, and close outside a band today". That is a new card with a pre-declared N, not a
  change to T07. Low priority: the lab's other breakout reads (T08, T09) are wrong-way at the week horizon.
