## Where it works and fails

- **The premium is a crash premium, and a weekly hit rate misses it.** 2018 and 2019 (-7.00, -7.48 bullish) are the
  years broad mid-caps fell while a few large defensives held; high-downside-beta stocks fell with them. The same years
  carried G02.
- On F&O names over one week, beta in any form is a bet on 2018-19, not a steady effect.
