## Where it works and fails

- **The edge is in the ranking, not the threshold.** %R above -20 fires on about 31 stocks a date (45,749
  bearish picks in the `all` selection), and that whole group is only +0.91 (t 2.45). The five closest to the
  top of their 14-day range are +2.89. So "overbought" as a threshold is weak; "most overbought" is not.
- **Steady:** positive every year, interval clear of zero, StepM superior in day/bearish.
- **Misses the provisional letter by 0.02%:** gross move about +0.23% against the 0.25% futures cost. It is
  the third oscillator fade to land just under (T04 -0.012%, T06 -0.026%, T13 -0.021%).
- **Bullish has no edge** and does not pay the delivery cost.
- **Mostly independent of the eligible day-bearish readings:** share of its top-5 picks also in D01C's 13.9%,
  E2A's 5.7%, T05C's 3.9%, A09's 3.2%; with T06 27.1% (`t06.md`), as two band-extreme readings would be.
