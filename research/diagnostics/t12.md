## Where it works and fails

- **The oversold up-cross is the lab's most reliably wrong-way bullish reading:** negative every train year,
  interval entirely below zero, larger than T05's up-cross (-2.86 on the day). A stock whose slow %K has just
  climbed back above 20 rises on the next day less often than the universe.
- **Different stocks from the other bullish readings:** share of its top-5 picks also in T04's 0.1%, T13's
  0.0%, D01C's 0.5%, T05's 5.9%. T04 (also "oversold") fires inside an uptrend on the RSI(2) extreme itself;
  T12 fires one step later, on the recovery from it, with no trend filter.
- **The bearish down-cross has no edge.**
