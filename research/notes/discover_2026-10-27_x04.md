# Discover batch, 27 Oct 2026 (IST): X04 Quantpedia and the papers behind it

Five weekly anomalies from the academic literature that Quantpedia (X04) catalogues, chosen because they are
price-only (ohlcv), fit the week horizon, and measure something no existing row does. Two were confirmed on
Quantpedia's own strategy pages by search on 27 Oct (G01, G02); G03 to G05 are cited to their papers directly.
Each claim is the paper's own, summarised; none is assumed to hold on NSE F&O stocks.

| Row | Source | Why it is not a repeat |
|---|---|---|
| G01 Residual momentum | Blitz, Huij and Martens (2011); [Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/strategies/residual-momentum-factor) | A03 and I01 use total returns; residual momentum removes the market part, which the lab has never isolated. |
| G02 Betting against beta | Frazzini and Pedersen (2014); [Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/strategies/betting-against-beta-factor-in-stocks) | A06 and I02 rank by volatility; beta is sensitivity to the universe, and the two rankings differ for stocks with high idiosyncratic risk. |
| G03 Frog in the pan | Da, Gurun and Warachka (2014) | A momentum quality filter: the lab's momentum readings (A03, I01, T23) are weak, and this asks whether smooth momentum behaves differently from jumpy momentum. |
| G04 Realized skewness | Amaya, Christoffersen, Jacobs and Vasquez (2015) | A05 uses the single largest day; skewness uses all 20. A05/A05B hold the week-bullish draft (A05B), so a related but distinct reading is worth a trial. |
| G05 Residual reversal | Da, Liu and Schaumburg (2014) | A01 (week-bearish draft) uses raw returns; this tests whether the stock-specific part of last week's move carries the reversal. |

Not added: overnight momentum (A08 covers the overnight/intraday split), time-series momentum on single stocks
(the same as T01/T02 in sign), and fundamentals-based anomalies (no data).

Port order: G05 (closest to the week-bearish draft, A01) and G04 (closest to the week-bullish draft, A05B) first.
