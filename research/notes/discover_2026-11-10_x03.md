# Discover batch, 10 Nov 2026: X03 101 Formulaic Alphas, fifth batch (F18 to F22)

Five more rows from Kakushadze (2016), "101 Formulaic Alphas", Wilmott 84, [arXiv:1601.00991](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.00991).
Formulas were checked against the paper's appendix A.1 on 10 Nov before the rows were written; the catalog text is the
lab's own summary, no text or code is copied. All five need only ohlcv (no VWAP).

| Row | Alpha | Horizon | Why it is worth a trial |
|---|---|---|---|
| F18 | #1 | day | Timing rather than size: where in the last 5 sessions the downside volatility (or, on up days, the price) peaked. No lab card reads the position of a recent extreme except F17's 10-session high recency, which was a null bearish. |
| F19 | #18 | day | The intraday body (close minus open) with its own 5-session volatility, faded. A02/F11/F13's fade family reads close-to-close moves; this reads the open-to-close bar, which the bhavcopy gives directly. |
| F20 | #35 | week | Three 16-32 session time-series ranks: heavy trading, a low bar level, weak returns scored bullish. A slower, multi-condition reversal for the week table, where only reversal readings (A01, F15, Q01C) hold an edge. |
| F21 | #37 | day | Today's down body scored bullish, tilted by a 200-session body-to-close correlation. A one-bar body reversal with a long-memory weight; compare with F01 (bar body share, null). |
| F22 | #26 | day | Turnover rising with fresh highs, faded. Price-volume correlations have been nulls three times on day (Q02, F04, F16); this one correlates time-series ranks of turnover and the high, and takes the 3-session maximum, so a clean null closes the family. |

Data notes: `volume` is replaced by traded value (`turnover`) where it is ranked or correlated (F20, F22), as in F09.
Price-unit terms are divided by the close (F18's price branch is the close over its 20-session mean; F19's and F21's
bodies and F20's bar level are divided by the close), so levels compare across stocks. One-signed products are centred
by the date's median (F20, F21). `Ts_ArgMax` (F18) is read as 1 for the oldest to 5 for the last session, first
occurrence on ties, as in F17. The cross-section is the stocks with an active future on the slice's last date (the
convention from the fourth batch).

Red flags for every row: published 2016 (train 2015-2020 is mostly pre-publication, so decay cannot be seen on train);
WorldQuant used them as small weights in a large book, not stand-alone signals; the paper gives no hit rate. F21 needs
200 sessions of warm-up (first picks late 2015).

Not added: #2 and #3 (volume correlations with the bar return and with the open: the Q02/F04/F16 family; F22 is the one
kept to close it), #22 (a change in high-volume correlation scaled by a price-level std), #45 (a cross-sectional rank of
the price level, which says nothing across stocks, as #4), the VWAP alphas.

Port order: F19 (extends the fade family to the bar body), F18, F21 (day), F20 (week), then F22.
