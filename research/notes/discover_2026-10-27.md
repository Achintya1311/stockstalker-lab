Three new catalog rows from the two collections already in the catalog (X02 Alpha158, X03 101 Formulaic
Alphas). Chosen for ohlcv-only inputs, day or week fit, and a low expected overlap with readings the lab has
already tested. Each gets a card with a pre-declared prediction when it is ported.

| Row | Source | Why it is not a repeat |
|---|---|---|
| Q07 RESI20 | X02 Alpha158 | Distance from the stock's own 20-day trend line. A01/A02 fade raw moves and Q05 scores trend smoothness; RESI asks where today's close sits relative to the trend, whatever the trend's sign. First look for a pullback-within-trend effect. |
| F01 Alpha#101 | X03 | Body share of the last bar's range. Q06 scores the wicks; on a bar with small wicks the two readings agree in sign, and on a long-wick bar they can disagree, so its overlap with Q06 is worth measuring before any combination. |
| F02 Alpha#12 | X03 | One-day reversal signed by the volume change. A02 fades extreme days regardless of volume and E2A needs rising OI; F02 uses only the volume change and fires on every stock. |

Not added: Alpha158 QTLU/QTLD (close against its 20-day quantiles), which is the Bollinger %B question T06
already answered; SUMP/SUMN/SUMD, which is RSI (T03); VMA/VSTD, which only rescale volume and carry no
direction on their own.

Source for X03: Kakushadze, Z. (2016), "101 Formulaic Alphas", Wilmott 84, arXiv:1601.00991. Formulas are
re-implemented from the paper's text (SPEC.md section 5); no code is copied. VWAP, which many of the 101 need,
is not in the data; F01 and F02 do not use it.
