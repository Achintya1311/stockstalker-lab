# Discover batch, 30 Oct 2026 (IST): X05 Open Source Asset Pricing, price and volume subset

Six predictors from the Chen and Zimmermann (2022) replication set ([openassetpricing.com](https://www.openassetpricing.com/))
that need only daily prices and traded value, each cited to its original paper. Chosen after G01-G05 (`research/diagnostics/g01.md`,
`g03.md`): week-horizon momentum readings keep beating the market line on hit rate but not the cost, so this batch leans to
signals about **risk and trading activity** (which pick stocks that move more) rather than more momentum variants. Each claim is
the paper's own, summarised; US and mostly monthly, so none is assumed to hold on NSE F&O stocks over one week.

| Row | Source | Why it is not a repeat |
|---|---|---|
| H01 Amihud illiquidity | Amihud (2002), J. Financial Markets | No existing row uses price impact per rupee traded. A07 uses volume spikes, not impact. |
| H02 Idiosyncratic volatility | Ang, Hodrick, Xing and Zhang (2006), J. Finance | A06/I02 rank total volatility; G02 ranks beta. This ranks the stock-specific part only. |
| H03 Variability of turnover | Chordia, Subrahmanyam and Anshuman (2001), J. Financial Economics | Volume rows (A07, Q01-Q02, T20) use the level or the change, not its variability. |
| H04 Return seasonality | Heston and Sadka (2008), J. Financial Economics | No calendar row looks at a stock's own same-month history (C02 is turn of month for everyone). |
| H05 Intermediate momentum | Novy-Marx (2012), J. Financial Economics | A03 and G01 include the recent 6 months; this tests whether the older half carries the signal. |
| H06 Downside beta | Ang, Chen and Xing (2006), Review of Financial Studies | G02 uses full beta; this uses down-market days only, and predicts the opposite sign (high downside beta bullish). |

Red flags for every row: the papers are US monthly; F&O stocks are large and liquid, which narrows H01's and H03's spread; H04
has at most four earlier years on train and abstains for 2015-2016; H05 and H06 need a year of history. Each port will pre-declare
its prediction and the same "helps" rule as G01-G05.

Not added: long-run reversal (36-13 months, leaves one train year), industry momentum (no sector data), and the
fundamentals-based predictors (no data).

Port order: H02 and H03 first (closest to "larger moves" on week bearish, where the cost is lower), then H06, H01, H05, H04.
